# Decisions
Small choices the design doc left open, one line of rationale each.

- **Flat genome** (`Record<LocusId, {a,b}>`, temperament loci prefixed `t_`): simplest structure that serializes cleanly and makes meiosis a loop.
- **Saves in IndexedDB**, not localStorage: a 128² world is ~0.5 MB, close to localStorage quotas; settings/keybinds stay in localStorage.
- **Game speed is not a sim command**: speed only changes how often `step()` runs, so it can't affect determinism or replays.
- **New worlds start at tick 360 (mid-morning)**: starting at midnight opened the game in darkness with everyone asleep.
- **Path cache lives on the World instance** keyed by `terrainVersion`; cost depends only on walkable/biome/wear>0.5, so cached and fresh paths are identical.
- **Path wear accumulation deferred to P7** (roads): the cost function already reads `wear`, nothing writes it yet.
- **Default clan archetype is `mixed`**: picking one from the seed often gave a single-colour village, which hid the genetics pillar.
- **Sleeping goblins do not witness** god acts: they can't see; keeps the reaction table honest.
- **Commands queued while paused are applied immediately** via `World.applyNow()` (logged at the current tick), so the hand stays responsive; replay applies them before the next step, which is equivalent.
- **One mesh per goblin** (not instanced): ≤ 300 goblins and each has unique proportions; instancing would need per-instance morphs.
- **Quick save uses its own `quick` slot**, shown at the top of Save & Load; autosaves rotate through 3 slots on `day_started`.
- **Replay viewer** wraps `World.step` to inject logged commands at their tick and pauses when it reaches the save's tick.
- **God hand scales with camera distance** (0.9–3 units) so it stays readable at every zoom.
- **Phase 1 committed in three batches** instead of one commit per step: it was built in one long session; per-step commits resume in P2.
- **Not every apple core gets planted**: a goblin pockets a core with chance 0.04 + 0.16 × industry, else tosses it; ground cores are only picked up when hungry. Keeping every core gave ~120 new bushes in 20 days; this gives ~30.
- **Planting slows as the nursery fills**: PlantSeed score ÷ (1 + growing apple plants near the village / 6).
- **Fertility draw is charged per finished growth stage** (0.15 × stage share), because per-tick draws round to zero under 4-decimal quantization.
- **`tileAt` floors fractional coordinates**: callers pass positions; flooring in one place avoids silent misses.
- **Worldgen mature bushes get ages 8–55 days** so they don't all turn old on the same day.
- **Village stockpile is a spot, not a building** (P2.3): `village.stockpile` + `stockpilePos` near the center; buildings like storage pits (P3+) can extend it later.
- **Chop target priority**: old apple bushes, then berry bushes, then fruitless mature apple bushes only while > 4 per goblin exist; trees wait for the stone axe (P4). Goblins chop until 30 wood is stored.
- **Memory merges untargeted entries within 3 tiles** (water spots) so a lakeshore doesn't fill all 60 slots; a goblin's first look covers 24 tiles (it knows its home area).
- **Drinking falls back to instinct** (nearest shore) only when no water is remembered, so founders on a strange island don't die of thirst.
- **Seen-gone plants are forgotten quietly** unless they are the current harvest target, which shows the `?` confusion on arrival.
- **Terraform power commands are one stroke tick each** (`god_power`, sent every 100 ms while painting); witnesses react at most every 30 ticks so painting doesn't freeze the village in awe.
- **Path wear starts now (P2.7)** instead of P7: walking adds wear, it fades 0.05/day, and crossing the 0.5 road threshold bumps `terrainVersion` so cached paths stay exact.
- **Renderer finds changed terrain by diffing a snapshot** of heights/biomes/wear rather than reading a dirty list from the sim (keeps the renderer read-only).
- **Uprooted plants stay in `plants` while held** (growth paused); replanting at ≤ 3 height on a valid site keeps them (hydration × 0.6 shock), otherwise they die.
- **Saplings in the Gift Tray are a `god_spawn_plant` command**, not an item type.

## P3.1 weather & temperature
- **Markov chain in compact form**: per-season weights + a per-state "persist" chance + `follows` (storms only out of rain/clouds, blizzards out of snow/clouds) instead of four full 8×8 matrices. Same semantics, a quarter the numbers to tune.
- **Forecast is real**: `weather.upcoming` is rolled one transition ahead (6 in-game hours = one transition), so the top-bar forecast never lies. Save v3 adds it plus `weather.cells` (local cells, used by P3.8 Rain/Warm Sun).
- **Warmth drifts toward a target** set by felt temperature (100 inside the comfort band, −6 per °C below it, band bottom = 10 − 8 × coldTolerance), with an hourly drift rate (0.15 down, 0.25 up, 0.4 up in sunshine), plus the §21 rain drain (6/h) and storm/snow/blizzard equivalents outdoors. A first linear-drain version froze every goblin in the first winter even with a hut; the drift form lets moderate cold settle at a lower warmth and only deep cold (winter nights outdoors, blizzards) reach 0.
- **Hypothermia** below warmth 1: 30 hp/day (×1.5 hatchlings/elders); hp only regenerates when warmth > 20. A `freezing` event drives a critical notification.
- **Shelter is positional**: a goblin within 0.9 tiles of a completed hut/stone hut/longhouse is sheltered (+8/+12/+16 °C, no exposure drain, can't get wet). No extra goblin state needed.
- **Air temperature blends seasons over the first day** of a season (not on day 0 of the world).
- **Rain sets plant hydration's target to 1** (instead of soil moisture × 0.8); puddles and mud are deferred to P3.6/P3.7.
- **Warmth only lowers mood** (below 50), so warm summers don't inflate mood.
- **Sandbox `set_weather` op** (click the weather in the top bar) is a logged command, so replays match.

## P3.2 seasons & snow
- **Foliage tints are per-instance colors** computed as linear-space ratios toward a target color (instance colors multiply vertex colors in linear space, so an sRGB-looking multiplier turned autumn olive instead of orange). Recoloring is in place, no geometry rebuild.
- **Season blend is quantized to tenths of the first day** so the terrain rebuilds at most ten times per season change.
- **Snow is a tile field updated every 50 ticks**: falls during snow/blizzard while the local air is ≤ 2 °C, melts 0.04/h per °C above 0 (×2 in rain, up to ×1.5 in sunshine). Local cells use their own temperature, so a Warm Sun melts a clearing.
- **Ground speed**: snow > 0.5 → ×0.8; worn trails (wear > 0.5) in rain → mud ×0.85.
- **`winterFoodEase: 0.5`** (temporary, remove in P5.6): berry bushes keep fruiting at half rate in winter; apples, growth and planting stop.
- **Season gameplay multipliers** live in `CFG.seasons`: planting ×1.4 spring / ×0.8 autumn / off in winter, exploring ×0.6 in winter.

## P3.3 buildings
- **Footprints stay walkable** but cost ×4 in A* (so goblins walk around huts yet can still reach the door/inside); placing a blueprint bumps `terrainVersion` to drop cached paths.
- **Building positions are integer tile corners**; a 2×2 hut covers the four tiles around its center.
- **Cooperative hauling without over-delivery**: a hauler only withdraws what is still missing minus what others are already carrying to the same blueprint (activity stage ≥ 2).
- **StoreItem leaves materials alone while a blueprint needs them** — otherwise haulers were pre-empted by StoreItem and put the wood straight back (an endless loop seen in the first browser run).
- **Contributors are tracked in `building.data` (`w<goblinId>` = labor ticks)** and each gets a `builds` deed on completion; construction materials are consumed, the storage then belongs to the hut.
- **Building skill grows 0.001 per labor tick** (a solo hut ≈ +0.5).

## P3.4 huts & homes
- **Residents are `building.occupants`** (claimed beds), not whoever is inside at the moment; shelter is positional (see P3.1).
- **Claiming works on planned huts too**: a homeless goblin takes a free bed in a finished or planned hut; only when none exists does it walk to `findSite` and plan a new one (and own it). At most 2 blueprints may wait for materials at once.
- **ClaimHome urgency** = 0.22 + season (autumn +0.15, winter +0.25) + 0.4 × cold urgency, damped by hunger/thirst.
- **SleepHome is preferred over the ground** (sleep score × 1.15) and restores energy at the `hut` rate (520/day, 2× ground).
- **Hut storage is a personal pantry** for surplus food (≤ 6 items); TakeItem checks home first. Materials still go to the village stockpile.
- **Chop's wood wish includes blueprint shortfalls**, so a planned village keeps the axes busy.
- **Hut spacing 3.6–7** (was 3–6): roofs nearly touched at 3.

## P3.5 planner
- **Subgoals are re-derived on every decision** (marked `sub`): they are a pure function of the root goal and the world, so re-deriving keeps `obtain` quantities in step with what the blueprint still lacks (a stale obtain(6) kept a goblin chopping after others had filled the hut).
- **The planned step gets ×1.5 focus and a 0.25 score floor** so cheap steps such as Explore still happen while needs are calm; urgent needs still win and, after 300 ticks of urgency > 0.85, the whole stack is abandoned (`goal_abandoned`).
- **Pre-emption time lives on the root goal entry** (saved state), not in a module map, so saves and replays stay bit-identical.
- **DeliverMaterial handles stockpile pickup itself**, so the planner only pushes `obtain` when the stockpile is empty. Partial loads are delivered as they come.

## P3.6 shelter, warmth, storms, mushrooms
- **SeekShelter is a normal action plus a storm interrupt**: rain/snow/heatwave are scored (0.45/0.3/0.25, damped by hunger/thirst), storm and blizzard re-decide at once (rate-limited to every 10 ticks per goblin). Once sheltering it stays available so the goblin waits instead of wandering back out; it ends when the weather clears or after 900 ticks.
- **Guests are welcome**: any finished hut within 25 tiles shelters anyone (no capacity for guests); only beds are limited.
- **Heatwave shade** = under a roof or within 1.2 of a mature tree; shaded goblins skip the ×1.8 heatwave thirst. No overheating mechanic yet.
- **WarmUp picks the cheapest of roof (distance), huddle (distance ×0.6 for friends, +8) and sunshine (daylight, clear/heatwave)**. Huddle partners must be standing still; if they walk off the action fails and re-plans.
- **Huddling is positional**: +2 °C felt per goblin within 1.1 (max +4), so hut-mates sleeping side by side warm each other too.
- **Storm damage follows §9.1 literally**: only buildings already below 50 % hp take 4 hp/h in storms; stone huts are storm-proof. Since nothing else wears huts down until P4 (raids) and P6 (fire), storms are harmless to healthy huts for now. The `repair` flag (hp < 70 %) waits for RepairBuilding (P4.6). Natural lightning is deferred to P6.2 with fire.
- **Mushrooms**: 25 at worldgen, then every 300 ticks up to 6 × season (autumn ×2, winter 0) random tries on forest/marsh tiles; dry weather only keeps 30 % of tries. They rot after 4 days; cap 70. Redcaps are never foraged (or remembered) until P5 knowledge brings the danger.
- **Test worlds clear worldgen mushrooms** like other scatter; an rng-luck test (berry accidental seeding) was given more trials after the new rng consumer shifted its stream.

## P3.7 weather FX
- **Particles live in a 64×26×64 box that follows the camera target** and are hidden per particle wherever `weatherAt` says it is not raining/snowing there, so local weather cells (P3.8) render as rain columns without extra code. With no cells the per-particle lookup short-circuits to the global kind.
- **Render-side randomness (Math.random) is allowed** for purely visual effects (particle seeds, lightning timing); the sim never sees it. Natural lightning *strikes* (damage, fire) arrive with P6.2 and will be sim events the FX can mirror.
- **Overcast is a smoothed scalar** (cloudy .45 … storm .92, heatwave −.15 = warm haze) that greys the sky dome and dims sun (−85 %) and ambient (−50 %): a storm noon reads clearly darker without going night-black.
- **Weather fog overrides the distance-fog setting**: fog/blizzard are gameplay (vision ×0.5/×0.3), so they show even with distance fog off.
- **Lit windows are shown while any goblin stands inside the hut** (within the shelter radius) and it is night; because the roof hides the walls from a top-down camera, a warm additive light pool spills past the door.

## P3.8 nature powers & casting UX
- **One `god_cast` command for targeted/global powers** (`power`, `pos`, optional `radius` and `to`); terraform brushes keep `god_power`. Validation lives in `sim/god/cast.ts`: unknown power, bounds, a direction target for Gust, and in Divine mode the unlock tier, favor and cooldown. Sandbox is free with no cooldowns (the optional sandbox-cooldown setting is not implemented yet).
- **Weather powers are local cells** (≤ 4, the soonest-expiring gives way): Rain and Snowfall last 2 h; Snowfall chills its cell so the air sits near −2 °C (snow actually settles out of season); Warm Sun is a `clear` cell at +15 °C that dries goblins on cast and keeps drying anyone standing in it. Clear Sky resets the global weather (cells stay).
- **Time skips are not a sim jump**: Fast-Forward Day / Hasten Season set `god.skipTo` and the game loop steps flat out (600 ticks per frame) until it is reached, so replays stay identical tick by tick.
- **Area powers are witnessed across their whole radius** (not only within vision of the center); Clear Sky is seen by every awake goblin. Reactions: growth/shower +4 faith +3 love; rain/warm sun +3/+2; clear sky +2; Gust targets +2 faith +4 fear; snow in spring/summer is an omen (+6 faith, +4 fear; superstition rolls arrive in P5).
- **Gust** pushes goblins (not held/airborne) and loose items within 1.5 of the dragged line up to 2.5 tiles down-wind, stopping at unwalkable tiles; no damage, a small mood dip.
- **Casting UX**: arming a card sets `armed`; LMB casts and the power stays armed; Esc or a right click without drag puts it away; global powers cast straight from their card. Wide rings (power radius) use a thin band and ignore depth so the whole circle reads over hills.
- **Temperature lens** colors tiles by local air temperature (cells included) on a −10/0/10/20/30 °C scale and recolors when the rounded global temperature or the cell set changes.

## P4.1 mining, sticks, tool durability
- **Durability lives on the stack** as `dur` (uses left); a brand-new tool has no field and counts as full (`usesLeft`). Tools stack 1, so they never merge; `addItem`/`takeOne` keep the field. A use is one finished job (a stone mined, a plant felled), weapons wear per hit (P4.5). Broken tools drop `breaksInto` parts (pick → stick + stone, axe/spears → stick, club → stone) and emit `tool_broken`. Repair waits for the workbench (P5).
- **Until Equip (P4.2) a carried tool counts**: `toolFor(g, job)` picks the best multiplier in the inventory; P4.2 will look at the hand slot first. Goblins keep their tools (never stored, dropped last) and only pick up a tool kind they lack.
- **Mine**: adults walk to the nearest workable rock within 30 tiles (one miner per rock, flint-bearing rocks slightly preferred), knock off one stone per 60 ticks (30 with a pick) until they carry 3, then StoreItem hauls it. Flint rolls per stone at the rock's `flintChance` (0.15 at worldgen). Rocks shrink visibly (`size` tracks `stoneLeft`) and vanish when spent. The drive is a village wish like wood: stone target 12, flint target 4 (at 0.6 weight), plus blueprint shortfalls, so stone huts and campfires will pull miners automatically.
- **Trees need a stone axe** (§11.1 "can fell trees"): with one, mature/old trees are the most valuable chop (8 wood + 3 sticks, stump left); trees take 2.5× the hits. The axe also doubles chop damage on bushes.
- **Sticks without an axe**: every 300 ticks, 4 random plants are sampled and grown trees drop a stick beside them at 12 % (×3 in storms), up to 30 loose sticks in the world. Stick wish (target 8) drives PickUp.
- **Planner** resolves `obtain stone/flint` to `mine` (or explore when no rock is known); only fiber/clay etc. remain impossible until their sources exist.
- **Tools tray tab** (spear, club, stone axe, pick): free in Sandbox; in Divine mode the tab is hidden and `god_spawn_item` is rejected with `locked` below god tier 2.

## P4.2 crafting, quality, equip
- **Recipes are a content table** (`sim/recipes.ts`): inputs, station (`none` = Craft by hand, `site` = building blueprint, `upgrade`), output or building, `known`, phase, work ticks. Starter knowledge (hut, campfire, spear, fence) is given to every goblin at creation; unknown recipes (stone spear, club, stone axe, pick, torch, stone hut) stay unknown until the knowledge system (P5.5) — kept in strict phase order, so in P4 only gifts or the Sandbox bring stone tools. Rope (needed by axe and pick) is defined as an item now; its recipe arrives with fiber in P5.
- **Inputs come from the pack first, then the stockpile's spare** — stock minus what open blueprints still need — so crafting never starves a hut. Consumption is exact; a failed finish consumes nothing.
- **Skill** `crafting` is a level that grows +0.25 per craft and speeds work by 15 %/level. From level 4 a craft is fine with 25 %. **Fine** = durability ×1.5 and half again of the bonus (pick mines ×2.5, weapons hit ×1.5).
- **Wants**: a weapon is wanted by how much damage it would add over the goblin's best (scaled to the strongest weapon, 0.1 + 0.16 × bravery); P4.4/P4.5 add a danger term. A pick/axe is wanted while the village wants stone/wood and the goblin has none. Before enemies exist, 3–6 of 10 goblins arm themselves by day 12 (balance runner now reports `armed`).
- **Craft is interruptible only while fetching**; once the hands are busy the piece gets finished (UtilityAction.interruptible may now be a per-goblin function), and StoreItem leaves the inputs of the piece in progress alone.
- **Hand slot**: `equipped.*` hold stacks (save v4 migration also grants starter recipes). Equip moves the best weapon (else a tool) from the pack into the hand, freeing its slot; the old item goes back into the pack. Jobs use the best tool from hand or pack. Rendering: the right hand shows the job's tool while mining/chopping, else the held item; loose cargo moved to the left hand; fine items get a gold band.

## P4.3 campfire & torch
- **Who builds communal things**: `PlanBuilding` (adult work action, one planner at a time) walks to the best communal site — the clear spot nearest the village center within 6 tiles, 2.6+ from other buildings and off the stockpile — and lays a blueprint with no owner; ordinary DeliverMaterial/Build finish it. The village wants a campfire once at least one hut stands. Fences etc. join `villageNeed` later.
- **Fuel is kept in ticks**: wood = 1800 (half a day), sticks = 450; cap 7200 (2 days); a new fire starts with half a day. Rain ×3, snow ×2, storm/blizzard ×5 burn rate. A fire runs out → `campfire_out` (the stub `fire_out` event stays for P6 tile fires).
- **TendFire** feeds sticks first (trees shed plenty), then wood, from the pack or the spare stock, 6 sticks or 2 wood per trip, one tender per fire, and only for the evening/night or when it is cold — by day the fire may burn low. The first balance run (wood-only, tended around the clock) cut wood and orchard growth noticeably (bushes 70→72 in one seed); sticks + evening tending restored it (70→96…112).
- **Warmth** +14 °C at the fire, linear to 0 at 5 tiles (fires don't stack), part of felt temperature; WarmUp prefers a lit fire (a step from the flames) over huddling. **Light**: inside 6 tiles of a lit fire night vision is as good as day vision. Repel radius (6) is configured for P4.6.
- **Torch** is a hand item with 8 uses, burning one per in-game hour of night while held; Equip holds a torch at night (over weapons) and swaps back by day. Vision ×1.4 at night; the scare radius (3) is for P4.4+. The torch recipe needs fiber (P5), so for now torches come from the Tools gift tab.
- **Render**: stone ring + crossed logs, a flickering three-cone additive flame scaled by fuel, a soft radial glow decal (no hard rim, ignores depth) and a pool of 4 point lights assigned to the lit fires nearest the camera (fixed count, so no shader recompiles). Held torches carry their own small flame.

## P4.4 Gnashers & spawn director
- **Threat budget** per night = (1 + day × 0.05) × moon − defense × 0.3, none before day 3. Moon runs from ×1.6 (new) to ×0.7 (full). Defense counts armed adults (0.5 each) and lit campfires (1 each) for now; fences and watchtowers join later. The whole part of the budget spawns, the fraction spawns with that probability, at random times over the first 60 % of the remaining night. In daytime fog an hourly 8 % chance (rising with days) adds a straggler. At most 20 enemies at once.
- **Spawn points** are walkable, ≥ 28 tiles from the village center and ≥ 16 from every goblin, forest preferred; the spawn point is also the Gnasher's home it runs back to. Small worlds with no such spot get no enemies, which keeps tests and tiny maps quiet.
- **Gnasher AI** decides every 10 ticks (staggered by spawn), acts every tick: flee below 30 % hp or when 3 awake adults are within 4 tiles; leave when it is light or it carries loot; flee after 3 bites. Otherwise it attacks a lone goblin within 8 tiles (outside, nobody within 4), or sneaks at ×0.6 speed to stockpile food, then to loose food, then to a lone goblin further away, else prowls near the center. Stealing takes up to 3 of one food and ends the visit.
- **Bites** wind up 4 ticks, then hit for 10 every 14 ticks via `combat.hurtGoblin` (death cause `combat`). A bite also counts as spotting the Gnasher, so a goblin asleep on the ground "notices" its attacker. Goblins don't react yet: fleeing, fighting back and fire repel are P4.5/P4.6 in strict order.
- **Balance** (32 days × 5 seeds): 31–51 Gnashers per run, 60–94 bites, no deaths (bites stop at 3 and hp regenerates), nothing stolen because stockpiles rarely hold food yet. Tick time stays under 0.2 ms.
- **Render**: hunched, spiny, goblin-sized beast with a toothy underbite, glowing eyes plus a soft additive eye glint (so it is visible at night), a crouched sneak, a lunge on the bite, and loot in its jaws. Enemies are interpolated like goblins. Feed: spotted and stolen are notable; bites are info.

## P4.5 combat, morale, injuries
- **Safety** drifts every 10 ticks toward a target: 90 by day, 55 at night outdoors, 85 under a roof; +15 roof, +20 inside a lit fire's glow, +10 armed, +8 per armed adult within 5 (max +16); each threat in sight −35 to −60, scaled ×1.3 (timid) … ×0.7 (brave). It falls fast (30 % of the gap) and recovers slowly (5 %). Shocks: a landed bite −20, a death seen −30 (both bravery-scaled). Low safety costs mood (×0.3 below 50) and shows as *scared* below 25. Nobody lies down to sleep in the open with a threat in sight or while scared.
- **Danger interrupt**: a threat in sight re-decides at once (every 10 ticks at most) and may cut any unforced action, even crafting with busy hands. Asleep goblins don't see; a bite (or miss) wakes them.
- **Morale** (§14.2): panic when safety < 20 and bravery < 0.5, or below 30 % hp — unless *veteran* (5 fights survived). A parent or child under attack in sight overrides panic. Children always run.
- **Flee** sprints ×1.15 (the Gnasher's 2.4 vs a goblin's ~2.5, so a limping goblin gets caught) to its own hut, else any hut, else a lit fire's far side, else 9 tiles straight away; it hides until nothing threatening is within 9 tiles or 40 s pass.
- **Attack** (adults) goes for whoever is biting kin, then anyone biting someone, then the nearest; score grows with bravery and hp, much higher when fighting back (+0.4) or defending kin (+0.5), and with fellow fighters. **DefendVillage**: armed or brave (> 0.5) adults converge on a *seen* enemy within 12 tiles of the center, damped once three already engage it (the Gnasher's crowd rule does the rest). **Guard**: armed adults with energy keep watch by the lit fire (else the stockpile) in the evening and night, one post per five adults (weight 0.3 + 1.4 × bravery × industry); a threat in sight turns a guard into a fighter.
- **Melee**: close to reach (fists 1.2, spears 1.5), wind up 0.4 s, swing, cool down 1 s. Hit 70 % + 4 % per combat level, clamped 60–90 %. Damage = weapon (fists 5) × (0.8 + 0.4 × size) × (1 + 5 % per level), fine weapons ×1.5. A landed hit gives +0.15 combat skill, wears the weapon, and knocks the enemy 0.35 tiles back. A struck Gnasher turns on its attacker unless it is about to run. Bites now roll too: 75 % − 2 % per defender combat level. Gnashers carry on biting up to 6 landed bites per visit (was 3) and keep at a foe that fights back.
- **Wounds**: a blow that leaves a goblin under half health wounds it 25 % of the time: a limp (speed ×0.7) or bruised arm (chopping, mining, building, crafting ×0.8) for 3 days, or an ear notch (+1 scar, max 3, already drawn by the goblin mesh). Sleeping in one's own hut heals wounds twice as fast. Save v6 turns the injury count into this list and adds `deeds.fights`.
- **Death** drops everything carried and held on the spot, shakes witnesses, and leaves a grave entry; graves render as a mound with a standing stone (burial, the cemetery and the grave inspector stay in P6.4). A killed Gnasher drops a pelt and a bone (and any loot); the killer's `deeds.kills` counts it.
- **Balance** (32 days × 5 seeds): 30–51 Gnashers, 12–24 bites landed, 0–1 killed, no deaths, 2–5 veterans per village. Control run without crafting: also no deaths so far, so the P4 acceptance target (≥ 30 % lost without spears) still needs tuning once fences, fire repel and the other enemies are in (P4.6–P4.7).

## P4.6 fences, repel, upgrades, repairs
- **Fences block enemies only.** A finished fence post makes its tile impassable to enemies (their A* gets a blocked mask, cached per terrain version, and their steps check it); goblins climb over at the ×4 cost every footprint already has. Gates (P5.7) will then make goblin crossings cheap. The no-corner-cutting rule makes a diagonal ring watertight. A Gnasher can't bite through a fence; a running (fleeing/leaving) one scrambles over anything rather than get stuck inside.
- **The ring**: once 3 Gnashers have been spotted (`threat.alarms`), a village with a lit fire and 3+ adults has `PlanBuilding` put up posts on a one-tile band around the center. The radius is fixed when fencing starts (farthest hut/fire/stockpile + 2, clamped 4–10, stored in `village.fenceRing`) so the ring never wanders. Posts go up 3 per trip, at most 3 unbuilt at once, continuing from the standing ones so the ring closes as it grows; each is 1 wood and quick to build (labor ×8). Water, boulders, other footprints and the stockpile leave gaps. In balance runs, villages ring themselves (47–56 posts) within the first month with wood to spare. Fence share of the ring adds up to 2 defense points to the threat budget.
- **Repel** (§11.1): a Gnasher won't come within 6 tiles of a lit campfire or 3 of a goblin holding a burning torch. Targets in those zones are skipped, and one that finds itself inside backs off to just outside. A struck Gnasher still turns on its attacker unless it is inside the glow. A night with nothing reachable ends with it leaving.
- **Stone hut** (station `upgrade`, recipe unknown until P5.5): an owner who knows it, by day, with 6 spare stone, turns its finished hut into the 60 % frame of a stone hut (goods inside go to the stockpile; residents stay; no shelter until it's done). Stone hut: hp 220, capacity 3, +12 °C shelter, storm-proof (already in config). Upgrades share the normal DeliverMaterial/Build flow; the def's `labor` multiplier speeds small jobs (fence ×8, stone hut ×2.5).
- **RepairBuilding**: finished buildings under 70 % hp get mended one piece at a time: 1 wood (stone for a stone hut) from the pack or spare stock, 8 s of work, +25 % hp; owners weigh it ×1.3; one repairer per building.
- **Render**: fence posts with two rails reaching halfway to each finished neighbour (geometry cached per neighbour mask, aligned to the grid); stone hut = fieldstone wall, lintel, thatch.
- **Balance** (32 days × 5 seeds): no deaths, fences 47–56 of 56 posts, 31–39 Gnashers, wood 30–43 left. Control without crafting: also no deaths, so the P4 acceptance tuning is still open (see Notes in PROGRESS).


## P4.7 Bramblebeast, Night Wisp, reinforcements
- **Enemy code split per kind**: `enemies/common.ts` (stats by kind, intent switching with fence-aware paths, walking, the spotted/alarm rule, `menaces`), one brain per kind (`gnasher.ts`, `bramblebeast.ts`, `wisp.ts`) and `enemies/index.ts` dispatching decide/act. Gnasher behaviour is unchanged.
- **Who counts as a threat** (`menaces`): wisps never (no fear, no flee, no fighting, no alarm, so they don't push fencing), a Bramblebeast only within 6 tiles or while it swipes at someone (it ignores goblins), everything else in sight as before.
- **Bramblebeast**: from day 6, rolled hourly (about 12 % a day), never two at once; it comes out of the forest edge out of sight, any hour, and heads for the point across the village from where it came in, by way of the center (±2). It walks straight; a building or fence in the next step gets smashed (20 hp every 1.5 s through the normal damage/destroy path, so a fence goes in 3 s, a hut in 7.5 s, a stone hut in 16 s); water and cliffs it walks round. Fire doesn't scare it. A goblin that strikes it gets swiped at (25 dmg, every 2 s, up to 4 swipes or until the goblin gets 6 tiles off), then it carries on; at 30 % hp it lumbers away without smashing. Kill drops: 2 pelts, 2 bones, 3 wood.
- **Mob rule**: goblins only attack or defend against a Bramblebeast when they hold a heavy weapon (≥ 20 dmg: club, stone spear) and at least three such awake adults are within 14 tiles of it; then they converge together. Everyone else lets it pass, and the timid and children flee when it comes within 6. In a test, four stone spears drive a full-health one off in about 7 s for one 25-hp swipe.
- **Night Wisp**: on full-moon nights (moon phase 0.5), as night falls, 1–2 wisps rise over marsh 8–30 tiles from the center (none if there is no marsh). They drift around a 12-tile circle and dart up to any goblin they see outdoors within 14 (×2.2 speed), hovering 2.5 off. Each 1 s near one (≤ 6), an awake, unsheltered goblin without a torch and not fighting falls under with chance 0.06 × (wanderlust + piety). The trance is a forced `follow_wisp` action: the goblin trails the light at 0.8 speed to a walkable point 26 tiles out (the light waits when it falls 3 behind). The wisp fades on arrival or after 90 s, leaving the goblin far from home at night with −8 mood for half a day. A bite or the god's hand breaks the trance; a lit torch within 3 scatters a wisp; dawn ends them all. In balance runs every wisp found someone (2–6 per month, no deaths yet), so the danger is being out in the dark, not the wisp.
- **Reinforcements** (`spawnReinforcements`, default on until P5): on every 8th morning (07:00) a village of fewer than six gets up to two adults of its own clan archetype from `founderGenome`, arriving 11–14 tiles out. It's stateless (tick-based) and needs no save change. Balance villages never shrink below six yet, so it hasn't fired there.
- **Save**: the new modes (`smash`, `lure`) and the `follow_wisp` action are new string values in existing fields, so they need no migration (still v7).
- **Render**: Bramblebeast = hulking vine-and-bark bear with thorns, a mossy hump and ember eyes; it lumbers, rears up and slams when smashing, and swipes with a foreleg. Wisp = additive glow sprite with a bright core and a trailing mote, bobbing at 1.2 m, from a fixed pool of two cold point lights (so materials never recompile). A goblin in a trance walks arms out, head up.

## P4.8 wrath, Gentle mode, anger, danger
- **Wrath powers** go through `god_cast` like nature powers and reuse ordinary systems, so replays stay exact. Lightning: 60 damage within 1 tile to goblins and enemies (a wisp just vanishes), plants split (wood if grown), buildings take 30, a campfire flares; fertility −0.3 on the tile; a 6-tile *dread* zone for 30 s. Swarm: a *swarm* zone for one in-game hour. Curse (goblin target, `targetId` on the command): mood −30 for 2 days, and each new non-danger task fails 25 % of the time (it idles and sweats instead). Summon: 2–4 Gnashers (marked `summoned`, they hunt even by day for 3 h) or one Bramblebeast; refused within the 12-tile village radius or on unwalkable ground (`too_close` / `bad_site`). Meteor: queued in `god.pending` and lands 15 ticks later: kills within 1.5, damage fading to the rim, destroys buildings, clears plants, lowers a crater (never below the water line + 0.02, so it doesn't turn meadows into ponds), scorches fertility, scatters 6 stones incl. 1 flint, and leaves a dread zone of r + 6.
- **Deferred**: lightning ignition and the meteor's ring of fire are visual only until fire spreads in P6; the meteor's 30 % star stone waits for trinkets (P5); Blight, Plague, Flood and Earthquake come with their systems (P6/P7).
- **Avoid-zones** (`god.zones`): every 5 ticks an unsheltered goblin inside a dread or swarm zone is forced into `scatter` (sprint ×1.15 to 2.5 tiles past the rim); a swarm also stings (mood −6). Zones expire on their own.
- **Thrown hits**: a goblin or item flying faster than 3 tiles/s that comes within 0.9 of an enemy deals (speed − 3) × 4 × mass (goblin 1.5, stone/flint 1, wood 0.8, bone 0.6); in Gentle mode a thrown goblin lands without harm.
- **Lethal preview**: `castPreview` lists goblins a lightning bolt or meteor would kill (hp vs. the damage at their distance); the UI rings them red with a ☠ marker and counts them in the cursor label. With **Confirm lethal casts** on (default), such a cast waits for a second click on the same spot or Enter; Esc or moving away calls it off. Summons can't be previewed (the beasts decide), so they don't ask.
- **Gentle mode** (`god.gentle`, settings toggle and wizard): wrath casts are rejected (`gentle_mode`), the Wrath button and the quick-ring Smite are hidden, the hand can't hurt.
- **Hotkey**: the design table says W, but W is camera pan (WASD), so Wrath is **Z** (rebindable), the way Nature moved to R.
- **Anger at the god** (`godHarmed`): when the god hurts a goblin (hand, thrown, lightning, meteor, curse), kin and friends (opinion > 50) who see it or are kin get faith +4, love −25, fear +20, `angryAtGod` for 8 days with mood −10, and show 💢 (or a nervous sweat if very pious). A kindness aimed at them (gift, gentle set-down, pet, apple shower, bountiful growth, warm sun) melts it at once. Reactions: lightning/meteor faith +8, fear +15; swarm +3/+6; curse target +5, love −8, fear +10.
- **Danger marks** (`threat.marks`, max 48, kept 3 days): spotted enemy 0.5, bite 1, theft 0.7, smashed building 1, beast swipe 1, wisp lure 0.5, a combat or divine death 2. `dangerAt` adds live menacing enemies (within 8) and the god's dread/swarm zones (0.8), and a lit fire's glow calms it by 60 %. The **danger lens** dims calm ground green and paints trouble amber to red; fire risk joins it with fire spread (P6).
- **Save v8**: `godView.angryUntil`, `god.zones`, `god.pending`, `threat.marks`.

## P4 acceptance tuning (undefended villages)
- **Control run** (`--no-craft`): besides switching off `Craft`, every goblin (and newcomer) forgets all Phase 4 recipes, so no spears, torches, campfire or fence ring; huts (P3) stay. `PlanBuilding` now needs the campfire/fence recipe known, which normal games always have (starter recipes). The verdict is the loss share over all seeds (deaths ÷ founders + newcomers ≥ 30 %); per-seed survival flags (extinction, housing) are only reported for the control.
- Before tuning, undefended villages lost nobody: a Gnasher turned on whoever punched it, so bites were spread over the whole group, and three bare-handed goblins scared it off. Changes:
  - **It doesn't let go**: once it is biting someone, being hit no longer makes it switch to the attacker.
  - **Thick hide** (`gnasher.armor` 2, the §14.2 armor term): fists do about 3 instead of 5; spears and clubs barely notice. Knockback scales with damage (full at 12), so a punch hardly shoves it.
  - **Unarmed crowds count half** toward its pack-shyness (armed or torch-bearing adults count 1; still 3 to send it off).
  - **Pack call**: a landed bite draws idle Gnashers within 10 tiles onto the same goblin.
  - **Wattle huts are no fortress** (`hutBreakIn`): a Gnasher can reach a lone goblin in a wattle hut; stone huts keep it out, and the fire's glow and the fence ring already keep it away. "Lone" now means no awake adult within 4 (sleepers don't help).
  - **Bites per visit** 6 → 10.
- Result (32 days × 5 seeds): armed villages 0, 0, 0, 0, 2 combat deaths; control 4, 14 (wiped out incl. newcomers), 2, 6, 6 → 59 % lost. The threat ramp stays at the design's 0.05 per day (0.1 wiped every undefended village).

## P5.1 relationships, chat, gossip, comfort, gifts
- **Relation** (`goblin.relationships`): one-sided `opinion` (−100..100), `familiarity` (0..100), a `bond` and `lastInteraction`. The bond is read off the opinion (friend ≥ 40, best friend ≥ 75, rival ≤ −40, enemy ≤ −75); at most two best friends (a third pushes out the weakest). Family bonds (mate, parent, child, sibling) are kept for P5.3 and never overwritten. `bond_formed` fires only when a bond deepens; cooling off is quiet. Kinship (`isKin`: parent, child or a shared parent) now lives in `goblin/social.ts` and replaces the two local copies.
- **Every contact** (`adjustOpinion`) also adds familiarity (1 + |Δ| / 2). Once a day, early in the morning, relations untouched for a day drift 1 toward 0, slower with familiarity (× max(0.33, 1 − fam/150)); grudge holders keep their dislikes; familiarity fades 0.5; empty relations are dropped; hut-mates gain +1.
- **Founders** start knowing each other (familiarity 30, opinion −5..20, so no friends on day 1); a band of newcomers knows each other the same way.
- **Social need** is active now: it decays 25/day × (0.3 + 1.4 × sociability) while awake, and refills 20/day in company (anyone not a rival within 3 tiles, checked every 10 ticks). Mood: (social − 50) × 0.2, −10 below 15, and the emotion `lonely`.
- **Chat**: wanted when social < 90 (score 0.05 + 0.8 × urgency, × 0.4 + 1.2 sociability). The partner must be free (idle, wandering or warming up, awake, not pressed by hunger/thirst/energy) within 10 tiles; friends, family and the familiar are preferred; rivals on either side refuse. The listener is pulled into the same action in a listening stage. 4–6 lines of 2.5 s alternate between them (`chat_line` events, drawn as topic bubbles). The topic is a weighted pick of what is on the speaker's mind (weather when it rains or is very hot/cold, food when either is hungry or the speaker is greedy, danger after recent marks, gossip when it knows things the listener doesn't, someone both know and it feels strongly about, a place for wanderers, the recently dead, a complaint when glum, trinkets). Both gain +25 social and +2..5 opinion (+2 more when the topic suits the listener; a complaint the listener doesn't share only earns +1).
- **Gossip** copies up to three of the speaker's most confident memories (bushes, water, rocks, mushrooms, places) the listener lacks, at 0.8 × the speaker's confidence and with the speaker's last-seen tick, so stale news travels as stale. Talking about a person moves the listener's opinion of them 20 % toward the speaker's. Talking about the dead gives both a small shared-grief lift; a shared complaint lets both vent.
- **Comfort**: a goblin not in a bad mood itself, not pressed by needs, goes up to 14 tiles to a sad (mood < −30) friend or relative nobody hugged in the last half day, hugs it for 3 s: mood +10 for half a day, the sad one's opinion of it +10 (+3 the other way), +15 social each.
- **Gift**: something to spare is food beyond the last bite (the most valuable first), else a flower; tools are never given. The recipient is a friend or relative within 15, the hungry first when it's food. Opinion gain = value × 3 (× 2 for food to the hungry), +4 for a flower, 2..20; +10 social each; a half-day cooldown. Hands full: it's set at the friend's feet.
- **Shared meal**: finishing a bite while someone within 3 is eating: +3 both ways, +6 social. **Fought together**: when an enemy is killed or driven off, everyone attacking it within 8 gains +8 both ways, and the goblin it was biting +25 toward each of them.
- **Deferred**: group chats round the fire (P6, with the fire as a social spot), the festival topic (P8), theft, prank and tantrum opinion effects (P8), the full Social tab with portraits and family (P5.8). The Social tab now lists up to 14 known goblins with bond icon and an opinion bar.
- **Save v9**: relationships gain `familiarity`, `bond` and `lastInteraction` (0, 'none', 0).

## P5.2 skills
- **XP, read as a level**: `goblin.skills` holds XP per skill (the §5 sketch), and `levelOf` reads it against cumulative thresholds 10, 30, 60, 100, 150, 210, 280, 360, 450, 550 (levels 1–10), linear in between, so effects grow smoothly and the UI shows one decimal. XP gains are × (0.8 + 0.4 × industry). Each whole level emits `skill_up`; levels 5 and 10 make a note.
- **Nine skills** (`SkillId`): forage, woodcraft, building, mining, crafting, combat, farming, cooking, teaching. Cooking and teaching exist and can hold XP, but nothing trains them until cooking recipes and the Teach action arrive (P5.5/P5.6); `RecipeDef.skill` already routes a recipe's speed, XP and fine chance to its skill (the wooden spear is woodcraft, stone tools crafting).
- **What trains what**: picking fruit 0.5 XP per item and mushrooms 1 (forage); every chop 0.3 and a felled plant 2 (woodcraft); every labor tick 0.04, a repair 1 (building); each stone 2 (mining); a craft 0.05 per work tick, e.g. 6 for a spear (its recipe skill); a landed blow 3 (combat); planting 3, watering 1.5 (farming). Rates were tuned so a month leaves goblins at level 1–4 in several skills with different leanings, instead of everyone being a forager or a woodcutter first.
- **Effects**: gathering, chopping, mining, planting and watering are 10 % faster per level; building labor +15 % per level (as before), crafting +15 % speed per level and fine items from 4 (as before); combat hit and damage as before (the level now comes from XP); miners spot flint +2 % per level; gardeners pour +10 % more water per level.
- **Specialization**: the utility score of an action is × (1 + 0.06 × level) of the skill it trains, so a goblin that happens to garden a lot becomes keener to garden, and the village slowly sorts itself into gardeners, woodcutters, builders and fighters. Personality still dominates (level 5 is only × 1.3); after one month most goblins are still generalists with a lean.
- **Save v10**: old skill levels are turned into the XP that reads as the same level. The old per-system constants (`crafting.skillPerCraft`, `combat.skillPerHit`, `buildings.skillPerTick`) are gone; all XP rates live in `CFG.skills`.
- **UI**: skill bars at the top of the Mind tab (practised skills only, best first); the inventory tab no longer shows a raw crafting number. The balance tool reports each village's specialists (best skill ≥ 2) and mutual friendships.

## P5.3 courtship, eggs, incubation, hatching
- **Lineage book** (`s.lineage`): every goblin that ever lived (founders, newcomers, hatchlings) with parents, name, skin hue, birth and death tick, so kinship checks and the family tree (P5.8) still work after a death. **Close kin** (no mating): parent, child, grandparent, sibling (a shared parent), first cousin (a shared grandparent).
- **Attraction** (§13.3): opinion ≥ 60, both adults (not elders), neither already mated, not close kin, and a free bed in one of their huts (or the same hut). Score = opinion / 100, × 1.5 in spring, × 1.25 for looks: goblins with wanderlust ≤ 0.5 like a skin hue within 60° of their own, wanderers one at least 120° away. That gentle assortative mating should keep visible colour lineages.
- **Court**: the most attractive candidate within 24 tiles, at most every 2 hours per pair. The courter walks up, hands over a flower (or crown/trinket, later) if it has one, and dances for 6 s. Welcome chance = 0.35 + (their opinion − 40) / 50, +0.2 with a gift, 10–95 %. Welcome: +4 opinion both ways and a heart; unwelcome: −1 and a sweat drop. Three welcome courtships with no more than a day between them make **mates** (`bond_formed` mate): both relations get the family bond, and the one without room moves into the other's hut.
- **LayEgg**: mates sharing a finished hut, both adults with hunger > 60, 12 days since their last egg, and today's fertility roll (a hash of the pair and the day, no RNG drawn, so scoring has no side effects): chance 0.35 × max(0, 1 − (goblins + eggs) / 40) per day, × 1.5 in spring. The layer walks to the nest, squats for 6 s and lays the egg; 5 % twins (two eggs, `twin` trait on hatching). The genome is made at laying by meiosis of both parents (the egg's shell is tinted with the child's skin hue).
- **The nest** is outside the hut door, under the thatch overhang (it counts as sheltered). Inside the hut the egg was invisible; at the door the player can see it, parents asleep inside still keep it warm (body range 1.8), and a Gnasher can still get at it.
- **Incubation**: two days of warmth (warmth ≥ 0.5) hatch an egg; cold time counts back down (faster the colder), and a day in the red loses it (`egg_lost` cold). Warmth: a parent sitting on it or asleep beside it = 1; otherwise (air − 5 °C) / 25 up to 0.6, +0.3 under a roof, +0.3 within 4 of a lit fire. In practice: a spring or summer day at the hut is warm enough alone, a night needs the parents home, a winter egg needs someone to sit on it (**IncubateEgg**: a parent sits until the hut alone will do, needs press, or 4 hours pass; one sitter at a time).
- **Gnashers eat unguarded eggs** at night: an egg outdoors or at a wattle hut (stone huts and fire glow protect it) with no awake adult within 4 is a target after a lone goblin; reaching it loses the egg (`egg_lost` eaten) and leaves a danger mark. Parental fierceness (bravery × 1.5) comes with children in P5.4.
- **Hatching**: a hatchling (age 0) with the parents' clan and home (not a bed of its own: children don't count against hut capacity), family bonds both ways at +40 opinion and 60 familiarity (parent/child, and sibling with any living goblin sharing a parent), a lineage entry and `stats.births`.
- **Childhood, minimal for now**: a hatchling can't hold food, so **FeedChild** has a parent with food (own pack, else one item from the stockpile) walk to its hungry (< 55) child and feed it from the hand (half portion for a hatchling, as at Eat). **FollowParent**: a hatchling more than 4 tiles from the closer parent toddles after it. Play, Learn, elders, natural death and adoption are P5.4.
- **Save v11**: `s.lineage` (built from the living, and from graves for the dead with unknown parents), `goblin.lastEggTick`, `egg.incubation`; optional `twinOf` on eggs and `court`/`courtTick` on relations need no migration.
- **Render**: speckled eggs in a straw ring, shivering when cold and rocking in the last hours; courtship dance (hopping in a little circle, arms up) and a nesting squat for laying and incubating; a "!" over a new hatchling. Notes: new mates, egg laid, hatched (with parents), egg lost (cold / eaten).

## P5.4 life stages
- **Fun** decays only for younglings for now (30/day × 2); hatchlings follow a parent and nap, and grown-ups get fun from pranks, music and festivals once those exist (P8–P9). Younglings' fun counts in mood like company does (−10 below 15, shown as "bored").
- **Play** (younglings): tag with another youngling within 16 tiles that is idle, wandering or exploring (friends and siblings first). The one who is "it" counts to three (1.5 s, hands over eyes) and sprints after the runner (×1.15 vs ×1); the runner zig-zags away and is pulled back to the middle of the game if it strays 6 tiles. A catch swaps the roles. Up to 42 s, +0.2 fun per tick, as tiring as work, +2 opinion and +4 social each at the end. Alone: hide behind a bush or tree within 10 tiles of home, on the side away from the village, for 6–14 s (fun × 0.6).
- **Learn** (younglings): walk to 1.7 tiles of an adult (kin first, then the most skilled) doing skilled work within 14 tiles and sit watching, up to 60 s; every XP the worker gains gives the watcher 25 % (`gainXp`), and watching feeds curiosity. Fights and guard duty are not lessons. The 2× recipe learning comes with the knowledge system (P5.5).
- **Light work only** for younglings: they can gather, forage, pick up, water and use the stockpile with one slot; building, delivering and gathering for goals are adults' work (planting a core was adults-only too until P5.5 gave it back to younglings). Younglings explore 5–12 tiles around home instead of 12–28 from where they stand.
- **Hatchlings** tire three times as fast while awake, so they nap about half the day.
- **Elders** score work × 0.6 and social × 1.3 (folded into the personality factor in the debug overlay); what they say in a chat counts × 1.3 (respect). +50 % teaching comes with P5.5.
- **Coming of age**: children sleep in the family hut without a bed (curled up between the beds). A new adult takes a free bed there if there is one, otherwise it leaves (`homeId` null) and ClaimHome finds or plans a hut.
- **Orphans**: when a child's last living parent (by bond, so adoptive parents count) dies, or an egg hatches after both parents died, the grown-up with the highest opinion sum (theirs of the child + the child's of them), +50 if their home has room (fewer than 4 children), +10 if they have a mate, becomes its parent (parent/child bonds, their hut) and the child gets the `orphan` trait (its effects come with the trait framework, P5.7). `isKin` now also counts parent/child/sibling bonds, so adoptive family is kin for fights, chat and mating.
- **Fierce parents**: an enemy going for a goblin's egg (a Gnasher in steal mode) counts like one biting its kin: no panic, no fleeing, +0.5 to Attack, and bravery × 1.5 (capped at 1) for Attack and DefendVillage.
- **Stand-in newcomers removed** (`spawnReinforcements`, P4.7): villages now grow only by eggs. The `immigrants` event stays for marriage migration (P10).
- Natural death and continuous growth were already in place (lifespan 112 days × gene × 0.9–1.1; scale recomputed from age every frame).

## P5.5 knowledge
- **Two kinds of knowledge**, one mechanism: recipes (`knownRecipes`) and cultural facts (`knownFacts`: `redcap_is_poison`, `gnashers_fear_fire`; `blight_spreads` joins with blight in P6.3). The basics (hut, campfire, fence, wooden spear) are known by everyone and never taught or lost; everything else starts unknown.
- **Discovery** (§15.1): once an in-game hour per awake goblin (staggered by id), for each unknown recipe: p = 0.01 × (0.5 + wanderlust) × (1 + 0.2 × level of the recipe's skill) × inspiration (×3 with the `inspired` mood, the god's gift in P5.9) × share of input kinds within reach. Within reach = in the pack, in the stockpile when within 5 tiles, or on the ground within 3; one input must be in hand and every kind within reach. So no one dreams up a stone axe before rope exists. "Curiosity" in the formula is read as wanderlust, the personality trait for it (the curiosity *need* is about exploring). One discovery per goblin per hour at most; the discoverer gets a lightbulb, +8 mood for a day and a critical note.
- **Teach**: an adult (or elder) who knows something a free goblin within 15 tiles doesn't, and whom that goblin likes (opinion ≥ 10), walks over and shows them for 300 ticks ÷ (1.5 if an elder) ÷ (2 if the learner is a youngling) ÷ the teaching skill's speed. It teaches what the fewest goblins know. Kin first, then those it likes. Score 0.08 + 0.2 × sociability + 0.12 × industry, +0.15 when two or fewer know the lesson, +0.1 for kin. Afterwards: teaching XP (5), +2 opinion both ways, and a `taught` mood (+3) that lasts two days and keeps it from teaching again until then, so knowledge spreads over days, not hours, and a recipe known by one or two can still die with them.
- **Observe**: every finished craft, and every finished building whose recipe is not a basic (the stone hut), gives each awake onlooker within 4 tiles a 2 % chance to learn it (younglings 4 %, and twice that again for a youngling sitting in `Learn` beside the worker).
- **Facts by experience**: a goblin that doesn't know about redcaps forages them like any mushroom (and remembers them, and may feed one to its child); eating one costs 25 hp (60 % for children, never below 1), a `sick` mood (−15) for half a day and a note, and teaches it the fact; each onlooker within 8 learns it with 60 %. A Gnasher backing off from a fire or torch teaches `gnashers_fear_fire` to each onlooker within 8 with 25 %. Redcaps are never stored, and those who know don't eat or feed them.
- **What the facts change**: only those who know Gnashers fear fire flee to a fire when there is no hut to run to, and they tend the fire 1.3× as keenly at night.
- **Founders** each know each fact with a 70 % chance (their clan's lore); hatchlings start with none. Saves from v11 (save v12) give everyone both facts, which is how goblins behaved before.
- **Lost knowledge**: when a goblin dies, any recipe or fact no living goblin knows is lost (`recipe_lost`, a critical note); `village.lost` lists them until someone finds them again.
- **Village panel** (Y, or click the village name): goblins by life stage, the basics, each recipe and fact with its knower count (red with the name when only one knows), lost knowledge. Chief, policies, beliefs and festivals come in P8.
- **Orchard care** (balance after P5.5): the new clubs and stone spears cost wood, and goblins without an axe get wood by chopping bushes, so one seed's orchard shrank below 90 %. Now goblins keep more cores to plant (10 % + 20 % × industry, was 4 % + 16 %), younglings may plant them, and no apple bush, old ones included, is chopped once the village is down to 5 fruiting bushes per goblin (was 4, and old bushes were always fair game).

## P5.6 fiber & preservation
- **Grass patches** (the worldgen grass tufts) carry a density 0..1. `CutGrass` takes a patch of density ≥ 0.35 within 30 tiles: 50 ticks for 2–3 fiber, density −0.3. Patches regrow 0.3/day in spring, 0.2 in summer, 0.1 in autumn, none in winter; the tufts are drawn smaller as they thin. Goblins cut grass while the village holds less than 10 fiber (counting fiber in rope) and someone wants fiber goods. Flowers bloom once (a mature flower holds 1 bloom, regrowing in 2 days); `PickFlowers` picks up to 3, hopping between blooms within 6 tiles. Felled trees drop 2 leaves (mature/old bushes 1), none in winter.
- **Fiber goods** are starter recipes (rope, basket, sleeping mat, loincloth, flower crown); only the leaf cape is discovered. A basket gives +4 slots for other things (its own slot is free: +5 total), a sleeping mat makes a night outdoors restore like a bed mat, a loincloth +2 °C, a leaf cape +4 °C and halves the wet penalty, a flower crown +0.1 to the courtship welcome chance and +4 mood when put on. Fine clothes give 1.5× warmth. Gear (tools, clothes, baskets, mats) is never stored or dropped, and `Equip` wears whatever beats what is worn. Clothes are wanted more when the goblin feels cold (×2). Saves from v12 (v13) learn the new starter recipes.
- **Spoilage** is exponential: every 50 ticks each food stack loses qty × elapsed days ÷ shelf life, with the fraction carried on the stack (`rot`) so small stacks rot too. Shelf lives (days): fish 1, stew 2, berry 3, flower 3, apple 4, mushroom 4, redcap 4, glowcap 6, leaves 8, apple core 12, apple mash 12, dried apple 30. Food in packs, pantries and the stockpile rots alike; apples on a drying rack don't while drying. Each storage pit (capacity 160) halves the rate for that much of the stockpile, the most perishable food first. The daily total is a `food_spoiled` event (a note from 5 up) and a stats series.
- **Winter frost** replaces `winterFoodEase`: nothing fruits in winter, and each piece of fruit left on a bush falls to frost with a mean of one day, so a village eats what it stored. The design doc's "remove winterFoodEase" is done.
- **Stocking up**: goblins harvest for the stores, not just their bellies, while the stores hold fewer days of food than the season's target (spring 1.5, summer 4, autumn 12, winter none). Score = base (0.15/0.3/0.55/0) × the shortfall. A stocking-up goblin strips the whole bush and walks on to the next laden bush within 8 tiles before storing. Days of food = stored food ÷ (goblins × 60).
- **Processing** (`Process`, adults, cooking XP): the surplus is stockpile apples over 2 per goblin. Priority: unload a finished rack, load an idle rack when the surplus is ≥ 6 (up to 12 apples), grind 2 apples → 1 mash (3 per trip) when it is ≥ 2. The urge grows with the surplus and when stores are thin, ×1.5 in autumn. A rack dries its batch in one day of dry weather (rain at the rack pauses it). Mash keeps 12 days (nutrition 55), dried apples 30 (30).
- **Stations** are site recipes the village plans like the campfire (after a roofed hut and a fire): one grinding stone, a drying rack per 6 goblins, a storage pit per 8, within 9 tiles of the stockpile. They are discovered, not known: standing at the stockpile counts as holding the inputs (their `near` kind is apples), and the chance rises 20 % per apple-equivalent spoiled that day (up to ×5), so rot gives the idea. Finishing one gives onlookers the usual observe chance, as does watching the processing.
- **Eat order**: goblins eat (and the stockpile hands out) the food that rots soonest first, then the most filling; preserves wait for lean days.
- **Pick-up order**: wood, sticks, food and gear before odds and ends (leaves, flowers, rope lying about), so goblins don't fill packs with leaves while wood rots on the ground.

## P5.7 workbench, watch, traits
- **Workbench** (4 wood, 2 stone, discovered at the stockpile with sticks about; the village plans one after a roofed hut and a fire, within 9 tiles of the center): a goblin with its inputs in hand walks to a bench within 18 tiles and crafts there ×1.5 faster. **Mending** (§11.3): a tool or weapon at half its uses or less, whose recipe the goblin knows, is mended at a bench, good as new, with half the recipe's inputs (each halved and rounded down; one of the first input if that leaves nothing), in half the recipe's time. Want = 0.32 × how worn it is. No bench, no mending.
- **Climbing**: goblins already crossed fences anywhere (a post tile costs ×4 in pathfinding); now they also move at 0.3× speed while on a post tile and are drawn a little raised, so a gate is worth having.
- **Gates** (2 wood, 1 rope; the idea only comes once a fence stands): once the ring has 8 finished posts, up to 2 gates at least 6 tiles apart, each on the ring tile with the most path wear (its own wear counted twice, plus the 8 around it), so gates land on the trails goblins already use. A post standing there is taken down (its wood back to the stockpile) and the gate blueprint takes its place. A finished gate blocks enemies like a post and costs goblins nothing extra to walk through.
- **Watchtower** (8 wood, 2 rope; after a fence): one per village, just inside the ring (1.5–4 tiles in), on the side the latest danger came from. One extra night guard climbs it (no weapon needed: a lookout, not a fighter) and stands on the platform; enemies within 3× its sight are spotted from there. A first sighting from a tower is a `tower_warning` (critical note; the plain "spotted" note is dropped): for 900 ticks children out in the open run home (Flee with the warned position as the threat). The defenders' head start comes from the enemy being marked seen early. The lookout's own reactions (attack, flee) use its normal sight, so it doesn't jump down at a speck on the horizon.
- **Acquired traits** (§7.7): `traits.ts` holds one entry per trait: earn rule (checked hourly per goblin, staggered), action weights (multiplied into utility like personality), a one-time personality nudge, and effects coded where they act. At most 3 per goblin; event-given ones (orphan, twin) count. The first five: **veteran** (5 fights on its feet: never panics, as before, plus guard and defend ×1.3), **scarred** (2 scars: Gnashers treat it as twice as far when choosing whom to bite, and it counts ×1.5 in the crowd that scares them off), **green thumb** (25 plantings: its plantings grow ×1.5; plant and water ×1.3), **orphan** (bravery +0.1, sociability −0.1, once, clamped), **twin** (twins start at opinion 65; a mood change of 4 or more echoes to the living twin at 40 %, not bouncing back). `grudge_holder` stays in the table without an earn rule until theft and pranks (P8).
- **Balance after P5.7.** One seed starved at the end of winter with ~230 apples still on the bushes all autumn: the harvest, not the orchard, was short. Three causes, three fixes. (1) Goblins whose best choice could not start (a station the village "needed" with no free site near the center) stood idle until their next decision, a quarter of the autumn day: the village now only lists a station when a site exists (cached per map version), and a choice that cannot start falls through to the next best (up to the third) before idling. (2) A trip for the stores strips several bushes, so its score now falls off over 80 tiles instead of 40 (a snack still over 40). (3) Autumn stocking up scores 0.65 (was 0.55).
- **Lean times**: eggs laid in late autumn hatch into winter and eat its stores. Pairs now wait in autumn until the stores hold 6 days of food, and in winter until they hold the days left in winter + 3. Spring and summer are unchanged.

## P6.1 health and disease
- **Config split**: health tunables live in `src/sim/cfg/health.ts` and are reached as `CFG.health` (config.ts was near the 300-line limit). Later phases add their own `cfg/` files the same way. Phase 6 UI strings likewise live in `i18n.p6.ts`, merged into both tables.
- **Disease course**: `Illness { id, stage: incubating | sick, progress (days), since }`. Incubation runs at 1 day/day; the sickness at the recovery rate: vigor `1 + (v − 1) × 2` (0.6–1.4), ×1.5 lying down (RestSick, or asleep in its hut), ×1.5 when tended in the last day. The mortality roll happens once, when the sickness has run its course, at `mortality × frailty`, where frailty `1 − (v − 1) × 2.5` (0.5–1.5) also scales every infection chance. Shivers can kill only hatchlings, younglings and elders. hp drains while sick (gut rot 50/day, fever 40, shivers 20) but never below 5: death comes only from the roll. Immunity after recovery: sniffles 8 days, spotted fever 64, none for gut rot and shivers.
- **Sources**: the doc's "rain-chilled" became "wet and warmth under 80" (4 %/h): wet goblins in this sim almost never drop below ~75 warmth, because they seek shelter. Gut rot: a redcap brings it on half the time (on top of its poison), and so does the unit of a stack whose rot carry-over is ≥ 0.7 (15 %), our stand-in for "rotten" since spoiled food vanishes. Raw fish joins in P7. Shivers: warmth ≤ 1 for 300 ticks (2 h). Spotted fever: once a day, chance 0.5 / 32 when more than 25 goblins live.
- **Spread** is checked hourly per sick goblin (staggered by id): contact rate per neighbour within 2 tiles, ×3 when the two are chatting with each other; hut-mates asleep in the same hut catch it at the sleep-share rate (×1.5 when 3+ sleep there). Only goblins showing symptoms spread anything.
- **Care**: `RestSick` scores by disease (sniffles 0.18, so the sniffly mostly keep working; gut rot 0.5, shivers 0.55, fever 0.65) or 0.3 when wounded below half hp; it lies down in the hut if one is within 30 tiles, else where it stands. `TendSick` needs opinion ≥ 20 or kinship, adults only, one tender per patient, once a day per patient; the patient's opinion of the tender +4. Being tended also speeds wound healing ×1.5, and lying down sick heals wounds like sleeping at home (×2).

## P6.2 wildfire
- **Fires are tiles** in `WorldState.fires` (`{x, y, intensity, fuel, since, cause}`; the old always-empty `fires` placeholder became this). Updated every 10 ticks: intensity +0.12 while fuel lasts (−0.12 after), fuel −0.05 × intensity, so a tree tile burns for about 40 updates (~400 ticks, under 3 in-game hours). Fuel is summed at ignition: mature tree 1.2, young tree 0.6, grown bush 0.7, flower 0.1, stump 0.3, grass 0.4 × density, a wooden building 1, forest ground 0.25, meadow 0.12. Stone huts, pits, the grinding stone and campfires don't burn; campfires still never spread.
- **Spread** to each of the 8 neighbours: 0.035 × intensity × min(1, neighbour fuel) × dryness × (1 + 0.8 × cos(angle to the wind)) × 0.7 on diagonals; dryness by season (spring 0.7, summer 1.25, autumn 1, winter 0.35), ×2 in a heatwave, 0 in rain or snow, which also damp a fire by 0.09 an update. Ground that burned in the last few days (ash > 0.5) can't catch again, so a fire front moves on instead of smouldering in place. Capped at 300 burning tiles.
- **Damage per update at full intensity**: plants 12 hp (trees become stumps, which char but stay and may sprout; bushes and flowers die), wooden buildings 8, goblins 5, enemies 6; eggs on a tile burning above 0.4 are lost.
- **Sources**: storms strike 0.5 times an hour (half within 24 tiles of the village): 50 damage within a tile and an 85 % chance to set it alight; heatwaves light a dry tile (fuel ≥ 0.5) with 4 % an hour by day; the god's Lightning Bolt lights its spot and a Meteor its rim. Tantrums (P8) and kilns (P7) will call the same `startFire`.
- **Firefighting**: `ExtinguishFire` (adults above 40 % hp, at most 6 at once) scores 0.3, +0.45 when the fire is within 6 tiles of the village edge, × (0.5 + 0.5 bravery + 0.3 industry); stomping takes 0.02 intensity a tick, which beats a tile's growth, so one goblin puts out a tile in about a minute of game time; 8 % × intensity chance per update of a 3 hp burn. A fighter whose own tile catches drops out and `EscapeFire` takes over (score 1.4, cuts any unforced action; runs 2–4 tiles away from the local fire's middle, upwind when standing right in it). Buckets (×3) come with P7.

## P6.3 flood, drought, blight, shooting star
- **Climate bookkeeping** (`WorldState.climate`, save v18): each hour of rain or storm counts; at dawn a day with ≥ 6 such hours is rainy, one with none is dry. Three rainy days in a row start a flood; six dry summer days a drought. Config in `CFG.disasters` (`cfg/disasters.ts`).
- **Flood = one water step of 0.03 height units**, which is exactly the sand band: every land tile below `waterLevel + 0.03` (~3 % of a 128² island) becomes water, remembering its biome. Goblins standing there wade to the nearest dry tile, eggs there are lost, plants under water lose 6 hp an hour, buildings 0.8 hp an hour (a hut survives about five days under water; a first try at 3/h destroyed two lowland huts per flood). Long wet spells still cost a village 0–2 lowland huts over two years, which it rebuilds. It stays at least 1.5 days and goes down at the first dawn after a day that wasn't rainy; the tiles get their biome back and +0.2 fertility. The water plane rises with it (eased, a little over the step so the drowned banks are covered).
- **Drought**: water tiles within 0.03 below the water line (the shallows) turn to sand and become walkable; soil water feeding plants is ×0.55. It ends at the first dawn after a day with any rain, or when summer ends; anyone out on the old shallows wades ashore. Wells and buckets (P7) will make it matter more.
- **Blight** (apple bushes only, as §14.4 says; mature or old): once a day in damp times (spring, autumn, or after a rainy day) a 6 % chance that a bush within 30 tiles of the village catches it at 0.15. It grows 0.09 a day when damp, 0.03 when dry; the fruit cap is cut by blight × 100 %; at 1 the bush dies and onlookers may learn `blight_spreads`. Each day it may pass to an apple bush within 2.5 tiles (0.35 × blight, ×0.3 on fresh ash). A blighted bush that loses its fruit becomes a chop target like any fruitless bush, which already helps. `CullBlight` (farming ≥ 3 or knows the fact; bushes at 0.25 or worse within 26 tiles) pulls it out roots and all, drops wood, and teaches the culler (and some onlookers) the fact. `blight_spreads` is never a founder fact (`FOUNDER_FACTS`); old saves keep knowing the two original facts only.
- **Shooting stars**: 0.4 % per night hour (about one a year) within 28 tiles of the village on open ground away from buildings: a crater 1.3 tiles wide (lowered 0.015, never below the water line), a `star_stone` ground item, and every awake goblin within 30 tiles looks up. The star stone is wanted by everyone (score 0.55–0.85 × distance) and becomes a legendary trinket on pickup instead of going in the bag. The god's Meteor now leaves one 30 % of the time (§17.5.4). Festivals and pilgrimages to the crater wait for P8.

## P6.4 grief, burial, graveyard, mourning
- **Grief** is one mood modifier per dead goblin, `grief:<id>` (so several losses stack and each can be eased on its own). Closeness: a mate counts fully, blood or adopted kin at least 0.7, others by opinion from 30 (0) to 100 (1); nobody with opinion ≤ 30 who isn't family grieves. Mood −10 … −30 for 2 … 6 days by closeness. The emotion reads `grieving` while some grief weighs 12 or more (after `scared`, before the mood-value emotions). Config in `cfg/death.ts` (`CFG.death`).
- **Graves are made at death** and hold the body's journey: `lying` where it fell, `carried`, `buried` in the graveyard, or `mound` (covered where it lay when nobody came within 4 days). The record keeps name, epithet, clan, birth and death ticks, cause, the deceased's most precious trinket as grave goods (the rest stay unowned until inheritance in P8), a eulogy slot for the LLM (P9) and, for 2 days, the goblin itself for Resurrect (P6.6). Old saves' graves become mounds.
- **The graveyard** is picked at the first burial: level, open, walkable ground 9–16 tiles from the village center away from buildings and plants; graves fill rows of 5, 1.3 apart, skipping spots taken by plants or buildings. Huts and communal sites keep clear of it.
- **BuryDead** (adults above 40 % hp who aren't sick): the body nobody else is seeing to, family and friends' own first; score 0.3 + 0.25 × closeness + 0.12 per day it has lain, × (1 − pressing needs). Walk, lift (40 ticks), carry at ×0.7 speed, dig 240 ticks of work. Anyone grieving the dead within 10 tiles at the burial has grief and its time left × 0.75. An interrupted carrier sets the body down where it stands.
- **Mourn** (younglings up): once a day per grave, for a grief of 4 or more within 50 tiles; score 0.24 + grief/60. Kneels 150 ticks; grief × 0.6 and its time left × 0.7. A zero-amount `mourned:<id>` modifier marks the day (hidden in the inspector).
- **Notes**: a burial (with mourners), a body covered where it lay, and a mate's heartbreak; other grief shows only as tears and the grieving emotion. The grave inspector shows birth (founders: "before the village was founded"), death, age, cause, grave goods, parents, children, who still grieves and the eulogy.

## P6.5 herbal remedy and the quarantine hook
- **The remedy** (`herbal_remedy`: 1 glowcap + 2 berries, by hand, 150 ticks, cooking skill) is a stack-of-5 item that keeps ~24 days and is never eaten. One dose per illness (`Illness.remedy`): the illness runs its course ×2 as fast (the doc's "recovery ×2") and, because mortality is rolled when the course ends, also ×0.5 as deadly — otherwise a faster course would save nobody.
- **Who takes it**: a sick goblin in `RestSick` takes the one it carries, or walks to the stockpile first when there is one in store within 30 tiles (stage 2); a carer in `TendSick` gives the one it carries at the end of its hour, and fetches one from a stockpile within its tending range first if the patient has had none. Crafters keep 2 in store (want 0.3 × shortfall), +0.35 when someone ill goes untreated and none is left.
- **The idea**: in these villages glowcaps are almost never carried (they grow in forest and marsh and are only foraged when hungry) and berries rarely stay in a pack for an hour, so "an input in hand" would have made the idea all but impossible. Discovery now also counts mushrooms growing within 3 tiles and berries on a bush within 3; and a recipe may name an `idea` input (here the glowcap) that sets a goblin thinking merely by remembering where one grows (×0.75 per remembered input). The remedy's idea comes ×2 as often as other ideas, ×3 again while anyone is ill. 64 days × 5 seeds: found on days 2, 6, 8 and 19 in four villages, never in the fifth; 1–2 doses made and taken in each (glowcaps stay scarce: whoever knows the recipe forages one when the store has none, as an errand that waits on hunger, thirst and sleep).
- **Quarantine** (`village.policies.quarantine`; a chief sets policies from P8, the sandbox drawer's World tab until then): a goblin showing a contact disease (sniffles, spotted fever) sleeps (`SleepHome`) and rests (`RestSick`, which then wants at least 0.45) in the nearest finished hut nobody lives in within 40 tiles; without one it rests at the walkable spot 5 or 8 tiles off that lies farthest from everyone (at least 5) and sleeps on the ground there. The doc lists `Quarantine` among the danger actions; it is this redirection of the existing rest and sleep actions rather than an action of its own.
- **Commits**: P6.5 and P6.6 share one commit (their changes to disease, state, strings and notes were interleaved in the working tree).

## P7.1 wildlife and sling hunting
- **Critters are light entities** in `WorldState.wildlife` (`Critter`: kind, pos, height `z`, heading, hp, `fed` 0..1, mode, goal, target, timer); no save migration was needed (the list existed and was always empty). They decide every 10 ticks (staggered by id) and move every tick in a straight line toward their goal; a step they may not take (water for hoppers, a fence, dry land for fish) ends the move. Step 14c, after the enemies. Config in `CFG.wildlife` (`cfg/wildlife.ts`).
- **Caps follow food** (`wildlifeCaps`): hoppers one per 6 units of grass (summed patch density, at most 18), chirps one per 6 grown trees or fruiting bushes (at most 14), fish one per 70 shallow-water tiles (water with land within 3 tiles, at most 40), glimmerflies one per 15 marsh tiles plus one per glowcap (at most 30). Worldgen starts hoppers and chirps at 60 % of the cap and fish at 80 % (`WorldParams.wildlife`, off in test worlds, which add their own). At dawn hoppers and chirps get 0.5 hungrier and die at 0; one that was fed (≥ 0.6) breeds with 18 % (hoppers) or 15 % (chirps) while under the cap; a kind below half its cap drifts back in from the wilds (25 % / 30 % a day, at least 20 tiles from the village); fish refill slowly toward their cap.
- **Hoppers** graze grass patches (−0.05 density a meal, +0.3 fed) and prefer sprouts and saplings (−20 hp a bite, so five bites kill one; planted ones raise `sprout_nibbled`), sit still at night, and bolt 7 tiles from any goblin or enemy within 4.5. Fences and gates keep them out (they use the enemies' fence mask); garden plots (P7.4) will too. **Chirps** fly at height 3 between trees, eat one fruit a meal off fruiting apple or berry bushes, peck seeds in the grass for half a meal when there is no fruit (winter), roost at night, and fly off from anyone within 5; a flushed chirp drops a feather 20 % of the time, and a perched one moults one with 1 % an hour. **Fish** nose about the shallows (P7.2 fishes them). **Glimmerflies** rise over the marsh at dusk, drift toward glowcaps within 7 tiles, and are gone at dawn; they are only scenery.
- **The sling** (2 fiber + 1 feather, by hand, phase 7, unknown at the start) is a hunting tool, not a weapon: fights still use melee weapons only (`ItemDef.hunt` instead of `weapon`). Its idea comes like any other (a feather in hand, fiber within reach), three times as readily with a hopper within 12 tiles. Each goblin without one wants one at 0.35 × the hunting want.
- **Hunt** (adults by day, healthy, not sick, with a sling and room in the pack): scores 0.32 × want × (0.6 + 0.4 bravery + 0.03 per combat level), where want is how short the food store is of 8 days (at least 0.25, and 0.4 × hunger urgency). The hunter stalks to within 6.5 tiles (hoppers bolt at 4.5), aims 35 ticks and throws: 40 % + 6 % per combat level to hit, 10 damage (15 with a fine sling), one sling use a throw. A hopper (10 hp) falls and leaves meat, a bone and a pelt, which the hunter picks up; a miss startles it, and the hunter tries up to four throws. Raw meat is food 30 that keeps a day and a half (cooked meat, 45, is P7.2's).

## P7.2 fishing, cooking, clay, the kiln, buckets and pots
- **Config** in `CFG.economy` (`cfg/economy.ts`).
- **The fishing rod** (2 sticks + 2 fiber, by hand, unknown at the start) is a tool (`tool.fishMul`, 30 casts, breaks into a stick). Its idea comes like any other (a stick or fiber in hand), three times as readily with a fish within 8 tiles. A goblin without one wants one at 0.3 × the hunting want × (1.3 − wanderlust), while the village has fewer rods than a quarter of its goblins (balance: without the cap every goblin made one and most were hardly used).
- **Fish** (adults by day, healthy, a rod, room in the pack): the nearest fish within 30 tiles that has a shore tile within 3 nobody else is fishing from. Score 0.3 × the hunting want × (0.7 + 0.5 × patience, i.e. 1 − wanderlust) × nearness. It walks there, casts (40 ticks) and waits for a bite: 150–420 ticks, shorter by 8 % per forage level and with more fish within 4 (up to twice as fast). Then 70 % + 3 % per forage level to land it: the fish leaves the water for good (they come back slowly toward the cap at dawn). The spot is remembered (`fishing_spot`). The first fish a village lands is news.
- **Cooking** is a fourth `Process` job: when raw fish or meat is in store and a lit campfire stands within 25 tiles of the stockpile, a goblin takes up to 3, cooks one per 90 ticks (cooking skill speeds it up) and brings the cooked food back. A goblin carrying raw fish or meat does the same with its own catch (at a fire by the store or within 25 tiles of itself), and goes straight to the fire when it has a full hand or nothing raw is in store. Raw fish and meat are eaten last of what is in the pack. Cooked fish is food 40 (keeps 3 days), cooked meat 45. Raw fish or meat eaten as it is gives gut rot 10 % of the time (`rawGutRot`).
- **Clay** is dug on marsh ground and on sand or meadow orthogonally beside water (`DigClay`, adults by day, 140 ticks at mining speed, 2 clay a dig, mining XP). Before anyone knows the kiln the village keeps 1 clay about, so that a goblin at the stockpile can have the idea; once the kiln is known it keeps 6, and a kiln blueprint short of clay counts as well.
- **The kiln** (6 clay + 4 stone, a site recipe like the drying rack, building skill) is a building (hp 120). The idea comes at the stockpile with clay and stone in reach, once the village has a campfire (a kiln is a fire with walls). Buckets and pots are **kiln recipes** (`station: 'kiln'`): their idea only comes once a kiln stands (`after: 'kiln'`, pots also want apples about), and `Craft` makes them only at a finished kiln within 30 tiles, walking there with the clay and a wood (the wood is the firing). After each firing a spark catches a plant or a burnable building within 1.6 tiles 5 % of the time (§10.3).
- **The bucket** (2 clay + 1 wood, 300 ticks, 60 uses) is carried as gear. Watering with one, the hands never dry on the way, and the same trip waters up to four more thirsty young plants within 3 tiles (five in all, the doc's "water ×5"); one use a trip. Stomping a fire with one takes off three times the intensity a tick; one use per tile put out. Each goblin without one wants one at 0.22 × (0.5 + industry), while the village has fewer buckets than a quarter of its goblins.
- **Pots** (2 clay + 1 wood, 300 ticks, stack 5) are kept in the stockpile. Each seals 10 units of stored food, which then rots at 1/1.5 the rate (shelf life ×1.5). Pits keep their share cool first; pots take the next share, most perishable food first. The village wants enough pots for its food stock, up to 6.
- **Rendering**: a clay dome kiln on a ring of stones with a glowing mouth; rod, bucket, pot and grilled fish meshes; icons reuse the fish and clay glyphs.
- **Thirst fix** (found in the P7.2 balance run): goblins only remembered drinking spots, and after a flood every spot they knew could lie under water, so the path failed and they went back to sleep until they died of thirst (three hatchlings and an adult on one seed). A remembered spot now counts only while it is still a shore tile; otherwise instinct (the nearest shore) takes over. Separately, a goblin whose thirst is below 15 no longer weighs the walk to water, so it gets up from sleep to drink.
- **Planning fix** (found in the same run): the village planned its stations (grinding stone, racks, pits, workbench, kiln) only while a wooden hut stood; a village that had turned every hut to stone early never built any, had nothing put by and starved in its first winter. Any finished home now counts.

Wood, sticks, stone, flint, fibre, clay, rope, bone, feathers and pelts are kept there; food and everything else (seed cores, leaves for the heap, ash for the paint, compost, remedies) at the old spot. `StoreItem` walks to the zone of the first thing it carries and puts down only what is kept there; the rest goes on a later trip. ## P8.1 full mood system, emotions, mood breaks, inspiration
- **Config** in `CFG.mood` (`cfg/mood.ts`); the code is in `goblin/mood.ts` (target, modifiers, emotion, work speed), `goblin/moodBreaks.ts` (breaks and inspiration) and `ai/actions/moodBreak.ts` (acting a break out). New Goblin fields are optional (`moodLow`, `moodHigh`, `moodBreak`, `lastBreak`, `lastInspired`), so saves stay at version 20.
- **Work speed** (§7.5): ×0.7 at −100, ×1 at 0, ×1.2 at +100, linear either side of 0. It comes in through `workMul` (crafting, mining, repair, culling, digging graves), the new `workSpeed(g, skill)` (skill pace × mood: chopping, gathering, foraging, harvesting, planting, watering, processing, cooking, stews, clay, paving, compost) and building labor (which already had a mood term, 0.95 + 0.25 × mood; it now uses the same curve, ×1 at a level mood). Village moods average 45–50 in the balance runs, so work runs about 10 % faster than before. Mood still multiplies work and social scores as it did (utility.ts).
- **Everyday modifiers**: each goblin has a favorite food for life (one of apple, berry, mushroom, dried apple, mash, grilled fish, roast meat, stew, hashed from its id); eating it gives +6 for half a day, set rather than stacked. A pious goblin (piety > 0.6) awake at night within 0.08 of the full moon gets +3 for a third of a day. A goblin out in rain or storm with warmth under 45 and no shelter has −5 while it lasts. Holding modifiers (`holdMood`) set the amount and extend the expiry, so a lasting cause does not pile up. The inspector names the modifiers (German too) and shows the favorite food, a break under way and inspiration.
- **Emotions**, most pressing first: scared (held by the god in fear, or unsafe), grieving, angry (angry at the god, or a grievance of −8 or worse: robbed, pranked, brawled, insulted; P8.3 sets those), excited (mood over 60 with a fresh lift of 8 or more on, or inspired; the relief after a break does not count), happy (> 45), sad (< −40), lonely, bored, content.
- **Emotions on the body** (`render/emotionPose.ts`): the head and ears follow the emotion whatever the goblin does (drooped and flat when sad, grieving or scared; pricked when happy or excited), and a goblin standing idle acts it out: a slump, a tremble and crouch, fists and a stomp, bouncing with arms up, the odd hop, looking about for company, a sigh. Ambient emotes add an anger mark or a tear now and then. A sulker sits with arms crossed and back to the village; a binger sits stuffing food in with both hands.
- **Mood breaks**: an adult or elder below −70 for half a day breaks (not again within two days). The kind is rolled by personality: tantrum 0.1 + 0.5 mischief + 0.4 bravery, sulk 0.1 + (1 − sociability), binge 0.05 + gluttony, wander off 0.05 + wanderlust. A **tantrum** is over at once: one random thing it carries or wears (not food) is smashed, or, with nothing to smash, its home loses 8 hp. A **sulk** lasts half a day: it walks a few tiles out past where it stands, away from the center, and sits there. A **binge** walks to the store and eats from it (whatever rots soonest), up to 4 items or until full, then it is over (with no food in the store it passes at once). **Wandering off** lasts a day: a walk to a spot 18–30 tiles from the center in a random direction, then sitting there; a quarter come back with a common trinket. While a break lasts every work, social and play action is dropped from scoring; eating, drinking, sleep, shelter and flight still run, and the break's own action scores 0.8 so an urgent need beats it. Each break ends in relief (+30 tantrum, +20 sulk, +25 binge, +30 wander, for half a day, `vented`) and is news.
- **Inspiration**: mood above 80 for a whole day lights a day of `inspired` (not again within four days), the same modifier the god's Inspiration gives: discovery rolls ×3 while it lasts, and the next craft comes out fine and spends it. Builds have no quality yet, so only crafts benefit. Its idea emote and the news show it.
- **Rates**: in normal play mood seldom drops that low (a probe of two 64-day runs had no half-day stretch under −70 and 2–3 full days over 80), so breaks come from hardship: grief, hunger, cold, wrath. The balance runner reports mean mood, breaks by kind and inspirations.

## P8.3 pranks, theft with witnesses, apologies, reputation, brawls
- **Config** in `CFG.mischief` (`cfg/mischief.ts`); the rules are in `mischief.ts`, the actions in `ai/actions/prank.ts`, `steal.ts` (Steal, Apologize) and `brawl.ts`. - **Pranks** (§13.5): only the mischievous (> 0.6) prank, once in three days at most, weighted 0.2 + 1.6 × mischief (§7.6). Four tricks, picked by what fits the victim (a stable day-to-day whim when several do, no rng in scoring): jump out at a sleeper in the open (safety −20, it wakes), swipe a non-food item from its pack and drop it 3–6 tiles away, slip a mushroom into its bed, daub its hut with berry juice (both only while the whole household is out; found at bedtime, −6 and −3 for three days; the paint shows on the walls for three days). A seen prank costs 1 reputation; four pranks suffered make a grudge holder.
- **Theft**: temptation = hunger urgency × 0.6 (food only) + greed × 0.4 + mischief × 0.3 + dislike of the owner × 0.3 − law (0.25 by default; the chief sets it from P8.4), + up to 0.2 for a dear trinket; tried above 0.36, scored 0.6 × (0.4 + temptation), weighted 0.5 + mischief, not again for three days. Three kinds: food from a hut's pantry or the best trinket off its shelf while the whole household is more than 8 tiles away, or a carried trinket from a sleeper in the open. Once started the theft goes through (not interruptible): interrupted walks were why almost none came off at first. Awake goblins within 7 tiles are witnesses: −15 opinion of the thief each, a `thief` memory, and the thief −10 reputation. With a witness the victim learns who at once (−25 opinion, −12 mood "robbed" for two days, angry); without one it only finds the thing gone when it lies down at home (−8 for a day, no idea who). Robbed twice makes a grudge holder. Stolen trinkets keep their owner chain.
- **Hearsay**: `thief` memories are passed on in gossip like any other news; the listener thinks 8 less of the thief and the thief loses another 10 reputation per new listener (§13.5: "reputation −10 per spread"). A victim who hears it this way learns who robbed it.
- **Apologize**: a caught thief with sociability ≥ 0.5 goes to its victim between half a day and four days after: the trinket back if it still has it, else food for food, else its least treasure as a gift; +15 opinion, the victim's "robbed" mood lifted, +2 reputation, the guilt settled. Old wrongs nobody owned up to are let go after eight days.
- **Brawls** (non-lethal): anger = how far opinion is past −50 (0 at −50, 1 at −100) + 0.4 for a known, unsettled theft against it in the last two days, × (0.5 + bravery), × 1.2 in a bad mood or × 0.8 in a good one; at 0.3 or more an adult in good health (over 60 %) walks up to a foe within 6 tiles and pulls it in. Fists every 20 ticks for 80, 3 hp a blow, never below half health; at the first blow a friend of either within 6 tiles may (35 %) pull them apart. After: −10 mood for a day each, −10 opinion both ways, −3 reputation each, 15 % chance of a bruised arm, any theft between them counts as paid back, not again for a day.
- **Reputation** (§13.6), −100..100: communal building +5 shared by the crew (homes, fence and gate pieces and bridge spans don't count, there are too many of them), a Gnasher beaten +8, a gift +1, a lesson taught +3, a tantrum −3, running armed from a fight others are in −4 (once in half a day), theft and brawls as above; it eases 2 % a day toward 0. Shown in the goblin inspector with the misdeeds.
- **Rivals and enemies** (§13.1): bonds already formed at −40 and −75 (P5.1); now rivals are the first picked for a prank or a theft (dislike weighs in both), and loathing past −50 is what starts a brawl, so enemies come to blows.
- **Balance** (64 days × 5 seeds and two `--no-preserve` controls): survival unchanged (0–2 deaths, pop 15–22); mean opinion at the end 20–41 (the runner fails a seed at ≤ 0); per village 0–27 thefts (most seen: thefts happen at homes, where others pass), 24–130 pranks (a clan of jokers pranks a lot; tried every day at first, now every three days), 0–13 apologies, 0–5 brawls; reputation ranges about −15..+95; tick 0.7–1.1 ms. Theft was tuned on a cliff: at a threshold of 0.30 one seed had 34 thefts in 24 days, at 0.38 one or none, so 0.36 with three days' rest.
- **Fixed on the way**: `Brawl.start` must plan the first path (the shared `reach` only replans when the target moves).

## P8.4 leadership: chief, policies, the night watch, justice, exile, headdress
- **Config** in `CFG.leadership` (`cfg/leadership.ts`); the rules in `leadership.ts`, the actions `Judge` and `Exile` in `ai/actions/judge.ts`. New fields (`exiledUntil` on a goblin, `ordered` on a guilt, the village's `watch` and the extra policies) are optional; save version unchanged.
- **Choosing a chief** (§13.6): score = reputation × 0.5 + age respect × 0.2 + the others' mean opinion of it × 0.3. Age respect runs from 0 on growing up (day 22) to 100 at day 80 (elders); "sum of others' opinion" is taken as the mean over everyone else (no view counts as 0), so it sits on the same −100..100 scale as the rest. Adults and elders at home (not in exile, not the shaman from P8.8) stand. The first chief is chosen on the morning of day 2 (day 0 nobody knows anybody); then on the first morning of each year, and the morning after a chief dies. The same chief re-chosen is no news; a new one is (`chief_chosen`, the first with no predecessor), with +8 mood for a day. No incumbency bonus: the design does not ask for one, and the acceptance wants chiefs to change.
- **Policies** from the chief's nature (§13.6), set when it is chosen: industry ≥ 0.6 → every work action scores ×1.1; bravery ≥ 0.6 → the night watch; sociability ≥ 0.6 → festivals ×1.5 (used from P8.5); piety ≥ 0.6 → the shrine first (P8.6) and quarantine for the sick (the P6.5 policy; the sandbox drawer can still flip it); mischief ≥ 0.6 → lax about theft (law 0.15), ≤ 0.35 → strict (0.35), else the usual 0.25. Shown in the village panel; the chief also on the "village at a glance" panel and as a badge in the inspector.
- **The night watch**: at dusk the two bravest armed adults with energy ≥ 50 are picked; they are always free to guard (no share limit), score +0.5 for it, stand till morning (not the usual 600 ticks), and while at the post the night does not pull them to bed. They leave it worn out (energy under 25), or to eat or drink (hunger or thirst under 25); off the post they go to bed like everyone else. The first version kept the watch out of bed all night whether guarding or not, and watchers who wandered off to eat never slept or ate properly again: one seed starved seven in winter (with the watch off, none).
- **Justice** (§13.5): the chief (`Judge`, a work action at 0.45) takes up a known theft not yet put right by a goblin within 40 tiles whose reputation is under 0 (its good name does not cover it), walks over and pronounces after a few words. Under the usual law a reputation of −30 or worse means two days' exile (−20 under a strict chief, −40 under a lax one; younglings are never sent out, nor anyone in winter); otherwise the thief is ordered to put it right: "give it back" if it still has the thing, "make it up" if not. An order makes `Apologize` available whatever the thief's sociability and at once (scored 0.9), so the thief walks over and returns it or gives a gift (P8.3's `makeAmends`). The thief −10 mood for a day, the victim +6, the chief +2 reputation.
- **Exile**: an exile inside 24 tiles of the center (village radius 14 + 10) heads out to a camp spot on its side of the village (`Exile`, 1.5, not interruptible); while exiled it may only feed, drink, sleep rough, forage, hunt, fish, roam and keep itself safe (`EXILE_OK`), so the leaving comes first and it keeps away from homes, the store, work and company. It is struck off the watch. After two days it simply comes home (`exile_ended`). Exiles who join a rival clan instead come with P10.
- **Headdress**: the chief wears a woven band with a fan of seven feathers (white, red, ochre) round the back of its head.
- **Justice thresholds**: first set at "under −20, exile at −45", justice never came up in 64 days on any seed (a thief's −10 a theft is soon earned back by building and gifts). Now under 0 and −30. A judged thief also keeps its hands to itself for six days (the usual rest after a theft is three): one habitual thief had taken 45 things in a run, 23 after.
- **Feuds** (a P8.3 fix found here): two enemies at −100 fought every day for 40 days, each fight −10 opinion both ways, so it never cooled. Now a fight costs −5 and both rest three days before the next (was one): 40 brawls on that seed became 7, elsewhere 0–3.
- **Balance** (64 days × 5 seeds and two `--no-preserve` controls): the first chief on day 2 everywhere, 1–3 chiefs a run (0–2 changes; the acceptance wants one change per three-year run); justice 0–11 orders a village (exile not needed yet); survival unchanged (0–2 deaths, pop 18–21, winter starvation 0–1 goblin-days); opinion 25–35; tick 0.85–1.1 ms. Variant runs found the starving watch: with the watch off, seed 5 had no winter starvation, with the policies' work multiplier off it still had seven.

## P8.5 festivals: the calendar, Starfall, merrymaking; instruments, the music circle, PlayMusic, Dance
- **Config** in `CFG.festivals` (`cfg/festivals.ts`); the calendar and effects in `festivals.ts`, instruments and the music circle in `music.ts`, the actions `JoinFestival`, `PlayMusic` and `Dance` in `ai/actions/festival.ts`. The festival on now and the ones held live in an optional `village.culture` (`cultureState.ts`, where the beliefs of P8.6 will go too); save version unchanged.
- **The calendar** (§15.3), each half a day: Sprout Day on the first morning of spring, the Sun Feast at mid-summer (day 4, or the first clear day after it, day 6 at the latest whatever the sky), Harvest Night on the last evening of autumn, the Long Dark at mid-winter (day 4, the solstice). A festival needs a venue: the longhouse for the Long Dark and in rain or snow, else the campfire nearest the center, else the longhouse. No venue, no festival (a new village has no fire on day 0, so its first Sprout Day is in year 2). Not with a storm overhead or enemies within 30 tiles of the center; the calendar ones keep trying for a tenth of a day.
- **Starfall**: the evening after a shooting star, if it fell within 70 tiles, the village walks out to the crater (no venue needed). **Merrymaking**: "chief personality changes frequency" — the calendar is fixed, so the frequency is an extra, an evening of music and dancing at the fire (0.3 day). It comes up with a 10 % chance each evening while the store holds five days' food, nothing has been held for three days and it is not winter, × the chief's festival policy (1.5 under a sociable chief).
- **Work stops** (§15.3: "suspend work for a half day"): every work action scores × 0.15 while a festival is on (not zero: a fire still gets put out), except gathering food (picking, foraging), which goes on as usual.
- **Going** (`JoinFestival`, social + fun, 0.8 × (0.7 + 0.6 × sociability), from within 70 tiles, younglings up): to a place in a ring round the fire (2.2–3.8 tiles; 0.6–1.5 inside the longhouse; round the crater at Starfall), spread by the golden angle. There it fills fun (260 a day, × 1.3 with music near) and company (220 a day), and every 240 ticks picks what to do: play if it has an instrument (75 %), dance (70 % with someone playing nearby, else 35 %) or clap along. Each festival has its own: on Sprout Day a goblin with a core in its pocket plants it on the way and the bare-headed wear flower crowns (a look, not an item); at the Sun Feast each comer has one helping from the store (a stew if there is one) and younglings race round the ring; the Long Dark is spent sitting, the oldest there telling the stories; Harvest Night's fire is built up into a bonfire (six wood from the store, two for the others) and halfway through the chief speaks (+4 mood for a day to everyone there, +3 reputation for the chief). The night pull to bed is not suspended: the less sociable go to bed early.
- **What it brings** everyone there for 300 ticks or more, at the end: +15 mood for a day (two for the Sun Feast), +2 opinion between every pair that came (+4 at the Sun Feast), reputation +1 (§13.6). Sprout Day: bushes within 24 tiles of the center grow × 1.25 for the rest of spring. Harvest Night: courtship × 2 the next spring. The Long Dark: grief moods of those who came are halved, the elders there +10 mood and +3 reputation. Starfall: curiosity +40, +8 mood. Personality drift (+sociability from festivals, +piety from Starfall) comes with P8.7.
- **Grown-ups' fun**: until now only younglings needed fun (P5.4). With music and festivals it runs down for grown-ups too, at half the rate (15 a day), and weighs lightly on mood ((fun − 50) × 0.1, so ±5). Pranks, music, dancing and festivals fill it.
- **Instruments** (§11.1): a drum (1 wood, 1 fiber, once there is a campfire) and a bone flute (1 bone), worked out like any recipe. About one goblin in four keeps one (wanted at 0.32 × (0.3 + 0.5 × sociability + 0.4 × mischief)); they are gear (kept, never stored, worn by use: a drum 40 tunes, a flute 60, mended at the bench), and Equip leaves them in the pack. **The music circle** (6 stone, footprint 3): a ring of standing stones and log seats, planned (once) when the village has eight goblins and someone plays. The idea comes while playing (an hourly roll, × 8 the usual chance), wherever the stones are: asking for stone at hand as well never happened, since stone is kept in the material zone and drummers play at the fire. Like the shrine and the totem it sits out with the stations (within 9 tiles of the center) or wherever there is room: the 6-tile ring round the center the communal buildings use had no room left for a 3×3 footprint by then, so it was never planned at all.
- **PlayMusic** (fun + social, 0.4 × (0.6 + 0.8 × (1 − fun))): of an evening (0.56–0.72 of the day), a goblin with an instrument and fun under 75 plays at the music circle (× 1.3 fun) or the campfire for 500 ticks; whoever is awake within 7 tiles gets 30 % of its fun. **Dance** (0.42 × the same): to someone playing within 40 tiles, for 360 ticks. Both weigh 0.5–0.6 + sociability and mischief.
- **Looks**: drumming, piping, dancing, clapping, sitting to listen, telling, eating poses (`festivalPose.ts`); the drum or flute in the hand while it plays; the music circle mesh; the festival's fire burns bigger (× 1.4, a bonfire × 2.2). Notes for each festival starting (notable) and ending (with how many came), the chief's speech; the village panel lists the festival on now or the next one, and the last four held; the village at a glance shows the festival on.

## P8.6 the shrine, the totem, Pray, superstitions
- **Config** in `CFG.beliefs` (`cfg/beliefs.ts`); the superstitions in `superstitions.ts`, the shrine and the totem in `shrine.ts`, `Pray` in `ai/actions/pray.ts`. Beliefs and the signs they come from live in the optional `village.culture`; save version unchanged.
- **Signs**: `World.emit` hands every event to `noteSign`, which keeps the omens and turns of luck of the last season (one of a kind an hour). Omens are things the village does or sees: a festival (when it ends), music of an evening, a prayer at the shrine, an offering on it, a redcap eaten, war paint mixed, a falling star, the full moon, a theft, a burial. Turns of luck: rain (good), storms, Gnasher bites and thefts ("raids"), falling ill, a death, a hatching (good), food from the sky (a gift, an apple shower, bountiful growth: good), the sky's anger (lightning, meteor, swarm, plague, curse, quake, summoned beasts, the god's blight and flood), blight, fire. Divine acts are luck like any other (§17.4), so the god can train them.
- **Forming** (§15.4): every 100 ticks each new turn of luck is paired with every omen of the half day before it ("X preceded Y"; the doc's example is "within an hour", which made the pairs too rare to ever come up three times). A pair counts once a day; once it has come up three times within a season, each further time it may become a belief with p = 0.3 × the village's mean piety, as the doc says. At most six beliefs at once and one new one every three days, so a village does not drown in them.
- **Borne out or not**: a belief starts at strength 3. When its omen comes and the luck follows within half a day, +1 (up to 5); when the half day passes without it, −1; at 0 it fades (`superstition_faded`). So an omen that comes every evening (music) is soon disbelieved unless the luck keeps coming too; a rare one (a falling star) can stay believed for years.
- **What a belief does**: a goblin's wish to do the omen is × (1 + 0.6 × piety) when it brings good luck and × (1 − 0.6 × piety) when bad: playing music and dancing, praying, stealing, mixing war paint, and (at the mean piety) the chance of a merrymaking; offerings: a pious goblin who believes they bring luck leaves another trinket on the shrine. When a believed omen comes, every awake goblin of piety ≥ 0.5 feels it: +4 or −4 mood for half a day ("a good omen", "a bad omen"; set, not stacked). Shown in the village panel as a plain sentence from a template ("Holding a festival brings rain.") with ☀ for good luck and ☾ for bad; formed and faded are feed notes.
- **The shrine** (§11.1: 4 stone, 1 trinket, site): trinkets are things with a history, not stackable items, so they cannot be hauled as building material; the shrine is built of its 4 stone and the trinket is laid on it by the first goblin to pray there with one on it (shown on the slab, listed in the inspector as offerings, up to six). Its idea comes to a goblin with faith ≥ 30 and a trinket on it (× 6 the usual chance × (0.5 + piety)); it is wanted once the village has five goblins and is three days old (a trinket is found on the first day, so the shrine came before anything else), first of all under a pious chief (`policies.shrine`).
- **Pray** (divine, from younglings): a goblin of piety ≥ 0.3 or faith ≥ 40, fed (hunger ≥ 40: food comes first) and rested, walks to the nearest shrine within 60 tiles, kneels 240 ticks (once kneeling it is not cut short: interrupted prayers were begun again and again, 2 211 on one seed) and comes away with +5 mood for two days (no praying again till it wears off) and +2 faith. Score 0.3 × (0.4 + piety) × (0.5 + faith/100), × up to 1.8 when it is low or frightened, × 1.3 under a pious chief, × its beliefs about prayer; personality weight 0.3 + 1.4 × piety. A prayer for something, answered or not, the favor it earns and praying anywhere in despair come with the faith system (P8.8). "Pray → piety": +2 faith, and from P8.7 a little piety (life drift).
- **The totem** (3 wood, 1 war paint): its idea comes to a goblin wearing war paint (× 10); wanted once the village has six goblins and Gnashers have come. War paint is put on as soon as it is mixed (P7.5), so the totem's pot is the exception: while a totem site waits on its paint, mixing it is wanted (0.2) by those who know how, and the pot is kept for the site instead of worn. Within 8 tiles, danger counts × 0.65 (like a fire's glow) and the safety a goblin settles toward is +10 ("village identity" has no mechanics yet beyond being the village's mark).
- **Looks**: the shrine is a cairn of flat stones with an offering slab and a bowl; the totem a pole of three painted faces with wings, in a ring of stones; kneeling with hands together and head bowed; a ✧ bubble as it begins.

New fields (the deed counters `prayers`, `tunes`, `cooked`, `watches`, and the personality `baseline`) are optional; save version unchanged.
- **Epithets** (§5: "Grubnok the Brave", "Fizzle Greenthumb", "Old Mogwick"): stored as an id (`brave`, `old`…) and worded by the UI per language ("{name} the Brave", "Old {name}"; "{name} der Tapfere", "der alte {name}"), on the inspector, the grave and in the feed. One starvation death on one seed, at the end of a first winter whose store ran out after a summer of raids; the other four had none.

## P8.8 the faith system: faith over a lifetime, gossip, prayers, favor, alignment, the shaman, offerings, the temple
- **Config** in `CFG.faith` (`cfg/faith.ts`), next to the doc's own numbers in `CFG.god` (decay, gossip share, conversion, favor rates, prayer length and reach, dependence share). The modules sit in `god/`: `devotion.ts` (faith over time, alignment's marks), `favor.ts`, `news.ts` (gossip), `prayers.ts`, `shaman.ts` (and the god's name), `faithLoop.ts` (their place in the tick, step 8c, and in the event stream); `reactions.ts` holds the reaction table, split from `faith.ts` so the AI can use it without the import cycle (faith.ts needs the AI loaded). The actions `MakeOffering`, `LeadPrayer` and `ShakeFist` are in `ai/actions/devotions.ts`. What lasts lives in an optional `god.faith` (`faithState.ts`) and two optional fields on the god view; save version unchanged.
- **Faith over a lifetime**: born with 20 + piety × 30 + 0.3 × the parents' mean faith (founders have no parents, so as before); younglings and hatchlings drift toward their parents' mean faith, 0.5 a day, either way. A day without seeing the god at work (or hearing of it) costs 1, never below piety × 20; love and fear settle back toward 30 and 10 (fear toward 40 under a wrathful god), 1 a day. So with no god at all (Observer mode, the balance runner) faith sinks to about 10 within a month, most goblins become skeptics, and few prayers or offerings come: "the god must stay present".
- **Gossip** (§7.8): the god's acts that someone saw live on as news for two days (the same act nearby within the hour joins the old news; the latest 24 are kept). A chat on gossip passes the freshest news the listener lacks: half of what a witness felt (×1.2 from a teller of mischief > 0.6), and it counts as having seen the god for the day's fading. Gossip comes up as a topic when there is god news to tell, as well as memories.
- **Skeptics and conversion**: skeptics (faith < 15) gain no faith from good things, as before; a kindness that falls on one directly (its target, or inside an area gift) converts it 30 % of the time: faith +25, a `converted` note.
- **Prayers** (§17.4): every prayer asks for something, chosen in order of need: healing (itself or a sick child), protection (Gnashers within 30 tiles of it or the center), food (hungry, or a bare store in winter), water, warmth, rain in a drought, the storm to pass, a lost thing back after a theft (or revenge on the thief it knows, if mischievous), forgiveness (unsettled guilt, or a pious goblin angry with the god), a child (a mate and no little ones yet), a mate; else thanks. One open prayer a goblin, for a day. It is answered by a fitting divine act within 8 tiles (or inside the act's area): food from the sky, or eating some; rain; clear sky or warm sun; healing on it or the one prayed for; lightning, meteor, swarm or a sanctuary near it; fertility, blessing; a shiny gift; a curse or strike on the rival; a kindness for forgiveness. Answered: faith +15, love +10, +8 mood for two days, favor +5, alignment +2, a heart. Unanswered after the day: faith −3, love −2, a sigh. Thanks need no answer and close quietly.
- **Desperate prayers**: anywhere, kneeling where it stands, when a need (hunger, thirst, warmth) has been under 15 for six hours, a child of its is sick, Gnashers are about at night, or in a winter famine (store under a day, hunger under 50); never with a Gnasher within 14 tiles (in a test a coward knelt to pray with one four tiles off instead of fleeing). Score 0.45 × (0.4 + piety) × (0.5 + faith/100). In despair it asks for what drove it to it.
- **Favor** (§17.4): each goblin's faith brings (faith/100) × (0.5 + piety) a day, counted hourly. Praying is ×3 for the kneeling time, given as a lump when the prayer ends (×1.5 near a consecrated temple, ×1.5 led by the shaman, ×2 for thanks). Offerings: a trinket's value × 2, food's × 0.5, when the god takes them a day after they were laid. A festival in the god's honor (Starfall, or one held at the temple) +20; an answered prayer +5. Capped at 100 + 15 × believers (faith ≥ 40), but never cut back when believers drop. Earned in every mode; spent only in Divine mode (P8.9). The balance runner reports it by source.
- **Alignment** (§17.4): powers move it by their definitions (now kept within −100…100); the hand's own hurtful drops −2 and gifts +1; answered prayers +2. Above 50 (kind): +5 mood for everyone (held day to day), merrymaking ×1.5. Dependence: once a season has seen 40 meals and over 30 % were god-given, everyone's industry drifts −0.002 a day (life drift, within its 0.2 band). Below −50 (wrathful): −5 mood, fear rests at 40, younglings' bravery drifts −0.002 a day, offerings ×2 as likely, the shrine and the temple planned first. Wish obedience comes with wishes (P8.9).
- **The shaman**: chosen daily (a fifth into the day) among grown goblins of piety ≥ 0.6, not the chief or in exile, by faith; a rival must lead by 10 to take over. Bone cap, horns and dark feathers (the chief keeps its own headdress). Leads a prayer every other day by daylight (0.3–0.7 of the day) when two of the devout are within 30 tiles: stands before the altar with arms raised for 300 ticks; the devout within 30 tiles who have not prayed since come (×3, even if they prayed earlier that day), and their prayers earn ×1.5. Beliefs about food from the sky or the sky's anger form twice as readily while a shaman lives. A chief of piety under 0.35 and the shaman lose 1 opinion of each other a day. Proclamations (the Voice's 40 % discount) come with the Voice.
- **Offerings**: `MakeOffering` (adults, faith ≥ 40, or a pious goblin angry with the god, which also doubles its wish): a spare trinket (with two or more; any for a temple not yet consecrated, for the self-blaming, or under a wrathful god), else food fetched from the store (apple, dried apple, berry, mushroom, cooked fish or stew; not with under two days in store), laid at the nearest shrine or temple within 60 tiles after a short kneel; once in three days. The shrine's first trinket (P8.6) and the temple's first two dress the place and stay; the rest are offerings, shown on the slab (food too) and taken a day later. Trinkets offered are gone for good (taken by the god).
- **The temple** (§17.4: 12 stone, 6 wood, 2 trinkets): like the shrine, its trinkets are laid in it, not hauled; two consecrate it. It is a prayer place (preferred over a shrine when both are in reach), a festival venue (Sprout Day and merrymaking, held there in the god's honor) and multiplies prayer favor ×1.5 within 10 tiles; tier 4 needs it (P8.9). Its idea comes to the shaman or a goblin of faith ≥ 60 while it kneels at the shrine; it is planned once the village is ten strong, two seasons old, four believe and two days of food are stored (three, in fact): the first try, with only a shrine and ten goblins asked for, put 18 materials into a temple on day 5 in a village where nobody believed any more.
- **ShakeFist**: a goblin angry with the god (§17.4) and of piety under 0.6 no longer prays; it shakes a fist at the sky now and then (50 ticks, +3 mood "vented", twice a day at most). The pious instead blame themselves: they pray for forgiveness and make offerings.
- **The god's name** (§17.4 "template"): once a shaman lives and three believe, the shaman names the god after the family of acts the village has seen most (the hand, gifts, the sky, storms and wrath, miracles) and its temper (alignment over 20, under −20, or between): "the Sky Grub", "the Gentle Hand", "the Burning Fist"… stored as `@family.temper` and worded per language; the Voice (P9) may give it a free name. No acts, no name.
- **UI**: the Prayers panel (P): cards soonest-to-expire first with portrait, the prayer as a sentence ("Snagwick prays for rain: the land is parched."), a time bar (red for despair), Go there, Answer (casts the fitting power straight at it: Rain over it, Healing Touch on the one prayed for, lightning on the nearest Gnasher, a Curse on the rival…; hidden when nothing fits, e.g. protection with no Gnasher about) and Ignore (off the list, for this session); answered prayers in gold below. The village panel: the shaman, how the god seems (kind, wrathful or neither, with the number), believers and favor. The inspector: the shaman role, the open prayer, "Praying in despair". Feed notes for despairing prayers, answers, conversions, the shaman, the god's name, group prayers, offerings and the temple. The temple is an open hall on a stone floor with a gabled thatched roof, bone horns at the gables and a stone altar with a bowl of embers; poses for kneeling with an offering, the shaman's raised arms and a shaken fist.
- **Balance** (64 days × 5 seeds, no god): faith fades to about 10 everywhere, so nobody believes at the end, the temple never goes up (it now waits for day 16, ten goblins, four believers and three days of food) and favor sits at 104–130 from the early days. Desperate prayers are not said with a Gnasher within 14 tiles (a coward should run, not kneel), and a prayer in despair asks for what is low (food, water, warmth). The raid-heavy seed 1 still loses one goblin to hunger in late winter, as after P8.5–P8.7.

## P8.9 Divine mode: tiers, costs, wishes, Divine Signs, the Voice panel, god profiles
- **Config** in `CFG.divine` (`cfg/divine.ts`): the tier table, the Gift Tray's tiers, the brush price share, the voice's tiers, what hearing the god does by tone, wish rewards, the wish tables (`WISH_ACTIONS`, `WISH_AGAINST`, `GATHER_FOR`). The doc's own numbers stay in `CFG.god` (wish bonus 0.35, durations, three wishes at most, defiance 0.25, voice costs, the shaman's 40 % off, overhearing within 8 tiles). Modules: `god/tiers.ts` (tiers, costs, `pay`/`afford`), `god/wishes.ts` (obedience, scoring, fulfilment), `god/speech.ts` (the `divine_speech` command), `god/answer.ts` (how a prayer could be answered now). Save version unchanged: the new fields (`wish.target`, `wish.defied`, `god.tierLowSince`, `llm.voice`, zone `ids`) are optional.
- **Tiers** (§17.9) are cumulative: tier n needs its own row and every row below it (tier 5 = a temple, 15 believers and average faith 75). They are checked hourly in Divine mode only. A tier earned unlocks at once; one lost holds 6 hours before it locks again, so a believer dipping under 40 for an afternoon does not flicker the toolbar (cooldowns are kept, as the doc says). "A temple" means a finished one (consecration is for favor and festivals).
- **Costs in Divine mode**: casts as before (tier, favor, cooldown); the Gift Tray costs item value × quantity, apples, wood and stone from tier 0, the rest of food, materials and life (saplings) from tier 1, tools from tier 2; terraform brushes need their tier and cost per-tile price × tiles under the brush × 0.1 per stroke tick (the paint tool sends one stroke a tick, so a second of painting pays the table's per-tile price once; the doc's "2 per tile per stroke tick" taken literally would cost hundreds a second); a rock is 8. Sandbox stays free, Observer may not act.
- **The voice** (§17.7): signs and words are one command, `divine_speech` (addressees or `all`, tone, at most two intents with optional targets, the source). One goblin from tier 0 for signs (tier 1 for the Voice), a group from tier 2, everyone from tier 3; 5 / 10 / 25 favor, the proclamation 40 % off while a shaman lives. Signs cost the same as words. Everyone who hears it (the addressees, and bystanders within 8 tiles of a lone addressee) gains faith +12 (+20 the first time ever, `first_voice`), feels the tone (kind love +6; stern faith and fear +4; wrathful fear +12, love −4; playful love +4, fun +10), counts as having seen the god today, and looks up; the fearful kneel, the loving dance, skeptics shrug. Only the addressees take the wishes. The memory entry the doc mentions is left to the event log (`god_spoke`) until diaries read it (P9).
- **Wishes** (§17.6): strength = obedience at the moment heard (the doc's formula, ×1.2 under a wrathful god). A wish adds 0.35 × strength to the matching actions' base score, even from 0 (the god may want wood when nobody needs any: that is the point), and multiplies the actions it forbids by 1 − strength (`stop` forbids whatever the goblin was doing; "leave alone", "make peace" and "share food" forbid pranks, theft and brawls; "hide" and "rest" wandering off). While a need's urgency is over 0.85 wishes wait. Gather can name the item (wood → chop; stone, flint → mine; fiber → cut grass…). Build names a building type the village knows (or a waiting blueprint): if none is marked out, the village marks one out where the planner would, and every addressee with strength ≥ 0.2 gets a planner goal to build it. Come here sends a stroll to the spot (Wander). Avoid makes a zone of 6 tiles that only the addressees keep out of (scatter as from a swarm, but not while starving). Fulfilment: the first matching action to finish (come here: within 3 tiles of the spot), or for avoid and leave alone the wish running out unbroken: faith +5, love +3, `wish_fulfilled`. **Defiance** (0.25 × mischief × (1 − love/100)) is rolled when heard: the wish lasts half as long, halves the very actions it asked for, and the goblin shakes its fist at the sky (`wish_defied`).
- **UI**: V opens the Voice panel; with goblins selected (or "Speak to all") the Sign wheel comes up: 21 wishes in three rings (work, safety, spirit) round a tone selector, with sub-rings for "gather what" and "build what" and a click in the world for "come here" and "keep away". The sign glows in the sky over the addressees for 4 s; up to three of them (the shaman first) answer in speech bubbles with mock lines by stance (obey, defy, doubt, babble, marvel), tone and personality; the panel logs the god's line in gold with how it was understood ("understood: gather wood · stern"), the replies with portraits, and kept wishes with a one-click Blessing. The log lives in `llm.voice` (annotation, outside the hash, last 40). Divine mode shows a favor meter with the tier in the top bar (hover: what the next tier needs), locks Gift Tray slots by tier, and the New World wizard offers Divine. The inspector's Mind tab lists a goblin's wishes and how well it heeds them.
- **A despairing goblin prays again only after half a day** (`CFG.faith.prayer.desperateGapDays`, set in P8.8 but never read): once the god answered a starving goblin's prayer, it was still starving the next tick and so prayed again at once, before it could reach the food. The new prayer test caught it.
- **A worship wish brings the shrine to mind**: in the god-profile runs two kind seeds of five never built a shrine (its idea comes only to a goblin carrying a trinket), so they sat at tier 1 for 64 days with every goblin a believer. A god who asks for worship where nothing stands to pray at now gives the shrine idea to an obedient addressee who lacks it, with a chance equal to the wish's strength. The player has a lever for tier 2 that is not luck.
- **God profiles** (`tools/godProfiles.ts`, `--god absent|kind|benevolent|stern|wrathful|chaotic`): a scripted god that acts hourly through the same commands (and `answerFor`, the Prayers panel's Answer), in Divine mode, or in Sandbox with `--free` (no favor, no tiers). The absent one never acts; the kind one answers what it can, blesses, feeds famines; the benevolent one (§20.4) is the kind one who also lays out an apple for most goblins every morning, drops food of a morning when the store holds under 3 days (checked hourly it rained 500–1100 apple showers a run, and the apples lying about drew one village into 221 Gnasher raids) and heals the sick and badly hurt; the stern one answers only despair, smites raiders and keeps the village at work with signs; the wrathful one answers only revenge and protection, curses the mischievous and throws lightning 7 tiles from a goblin for show (never within 5 of anyone: at 3 tiles its fires killed two goblins in one seed); the chaotic one casts one random power a day. Lightning at raiders needs no goblin within 4 tiles (at 2, two goblins died of it in the free runs). The runner's faith line now also gives average fear, love, industry, how far industry has drifted from each goblin's baseline, and the piety floor, plus the god's acts in any mode; the seed line gives the average population and the god-given food eaten.

## Phase 8 acceptance
- **Benevolent in Sandbox, not Divine**: the doc's `benevolent` profile (§20.4) predates the favor economy. In Divine mode a god earns about 22 favor a day before answering prayers, and an apple a goblin each morning costs about that, so the Divine benevolent god could not keep up its breakfasts and left the population where it was without a god (mean 15.7 against 15.8) while mood rose and industry drifted only slightly. Played free (`--free`) it sustains the larger, dependent village the acceptance describes. That a Divine god cannot buy dependence cheaply is kept: favor is the limit on doting.
- **Industry measured as drift**: the village's raw average industry at the end follows who was born (founders are gone after three years and children take their genes, not their drift), so it says little about dependence. The runner reports how far each living goblin's industry has drifted from its own baseline, which only the dependence rule moves: 0.02–0.06 down under the free benevolent god, 0 without one.
- **Absent faith below the floor**: the floor (piety × 20) only stops fading; younglings are nurtured toward their parents' faith and so can end below their own floor in a village that never saw its god. Kept, as it is the doc's nurture rule.
- **Housing at the first autumn** fails on about one seed in seven with or without Phase 8 (13-seed check to day 25: 2 against 1 with the Phase 7 code): a hut lost early on a raid-heavy seed. Noted, not tuned.

## P9.1 LLM providers, proxy, settings
- **No model name ships**: the settings' model is empty and the proxy needs `NIBBLETS_MODEL` beside `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (it refuses to start without both). The doc wants "a small fast model" by default; any name written into the game would age, and the player knows which models their key can use. The Mock needs none.
- **LLM mode is on by default with the Mock**: the doc makes the Mock the default "when no key is set" and wants every product testable offline, so journals and replies fill in from templates out of the box; the kill switch turns all of it off. Nothing in the sim reads any of it either way.
- **The request carries its product and facts** (`product`, `facts`) besides the doc's `{system, prompt, maxTokens, temperature}`: real providers ignore them, the Mock picks its template by product and fills it from the facts (and picks among variants by a hash of the prompt, so it answers the same prompt the same way). Each later product registers its own template with `registerMock`.
- **LLM settings live apart from the game settings** (`nibblets.llm` in localStorage, `llm/settings.ts`), because they hold the direct API's key; the language stays the game's own setting. Listeners (the scheduler, P9.3) get changes at once. Defaults and the scheduler's limits sit in `CFG.llm` with the other tunables (replacing the §21 stub, whose numbers they keep).
## P9.2 Event compactor, place names
- **Place names are sim data, their words are not**: the sim stores the place (spot, feature of the land, cause, a word index, the person if named after one) in the village culture and saves it; the name is put together in the player's language when shown (`llm/placeNames.ts`), so switching language renames every place consistently and the sim stays free of words. Naming hashes the tick, spot and cause with the seed instead of drawing from the world's random stream (it does take an id, so later ids shift by one per place), so it stays deterministic and replays the same.
- **What names a place**: a goblin's death, a slain enemy, a discovery, a festival's start and a falling star, each once per spot (nothing within 9 tiles is renamed; at most 40 names). Half of them carry the name of the goblin in the deed ("Grubnok's Hill"). The land word comes from the tiles around: water within 3 tiles makes a pond if there is little of it (under 60 water tiles within 8), else a shore on sand or a ford elsewhere; otherwise height against the neighbourhood (hill, hollow, peak) or the ground cover (wood, meadow, rocks, bog, dunes).
- **No place chat icons**: §15.6 has goblins "use" place names in chat; nothing in the sim reads them yet, so names appear on the map, in the feed and in every LLM prompt (the compactor says where things happened), which is where the player sees goblins use them.
- **The event log lives in the app, not the sim**: who saw an event is worked out once, when it is recorded (goblins within day sight, plus the witnesses the sim already lists for thefts and divine acts), and only a season and a day of it is kept in memory. It is rebuilt from nothing after a load, so a fresh game or a loaded save starts with empty diaries; saving it would bloat saves for text the LLM may never write.
- **Compactor scores**: a bonded goblin's death 12, one's own hatchling 10, a fight 9, egg laid 9, a discovery 8, the god speaking 8, injuries, festivals, thefts, brawls and mood breaks 7, down to routine work 1; an event only seen counts 0.6 of it. Identical lines merge as "×n" (four apple pickings are one line). The best eight are kept and put back in the order they happened. A place is named in a line only if it had its name by then.
- **Self in the lines**: the goblin is "I", and in object places "me" (German "mich" or "mir" by case), so the prompt reads as the goblin's own notes. German uses the Präteritum, where "ich" and "er" share the verb form, so one template serves both.
## P9.3 Prompts, sanitizer, scheduler, store
- **Instructions in English, facts in the game's language**: the system prompt is §19.5's word for word, ending "Write in English" or "Write in German"; the diary, dialogue and bark prompts keep their instructions in English (models follow those best) while every fact in them (feelings, season, the day's lines, place names) is already worded in the player's language by the compactor.
- **Prompt injection**: the only text in a prompt the player can type is names (the village now; the god and the Voice later). Everything taken from the world passes `cleanInput`: one line, no control characters, no braces, angle brackets or backticks, 40 characters for a name and 160 for an event line.
- **Which names an answer may use**: the caller passes every goblin name the world knows (living and dead) and the ones in the scene; a reply naming a known goblin outside the scene is refused and the Mock's text used instead (§19.6 "remove any name not in the allowed set → fallback to mock text"). Made-up words that are not goblin names pass, since goblin slang is wanted and German capitalizes every noun.
- **Budgets**: a product's count is charged when its request starts, not when it is offered, but an offer is refused while the day's count plus those already waiting would pass the budget. Tokens are estimated (prompt characters / 4 plus the most it may answer), since providers here return only text. The Voice has its own 4k sub-cap and is checked only against it, so a day full of diaries cannot silence the god's words; its tokens still count toward the day's total, leaving less for stories.
- **The Mock is local**: no concurrency or rate limit for it (at 64× a day lasts under 6 s, and the Mock answers at once); budgets and counts apply the same, so offline play paces journals as the real thing would.
- **Failures**: three requests in a row that failed even after their retry rest LLM mode for 10 in-game minutes (`pauseTicks` 25); jobs wait out the rest in the queue (stale ones expire) instead of being dropped. A request that failed for good, or an answer the sanitizer refused, still gets the Mock's text, so a diary never stays blank; refusals are not failures.
- **Store limits**: 48 diaries a goblin (six seasons) and 1000 journal entries in all, oldest diaries going first (eulogies, chronicles and books stay); the last 60 lines of speech. The old `speechText` map stays for save compatibility; journals were always empty before, so no migration is needed.
## P9.4 Speech, diaries, journal
- **The director lives in the app**: it reads the sim's events after each frame's batch (never inside a step) and writes only into `world.llm`; replays skip it (they are read-only and would write a second set of diaries). The one-day test runs two worlds from one seed, one with the director and the Mock writing diaries, and gets the same state hash.
- **Dialogue trigger**: "both have an interesting recent event or strong opinion" reads as: both goblins' best line today scores 5 or more (a building finished, a gift, a fight, a death…), or either one's opinion of the other is ±50 or beyond. The news each brings is its best line of the day.
- **Barks**: the victor of a slain enemy, the goblin who worked out a recipe, the first living parent of a hatchling, and for a death the bonded goblin who thought most of the dead. One per goblin an hour; a bark is no use after 10 s of game time, so at high speed with a slow model most are dropped, as §19.1 allows.
- **Bubbles**: barks show at once for 3.5 s; a dialogue shows a line every 1.5 s, each until its speaker's next line (the doc's "in sync with the chat animation"; the answer arrives after the chat has begun, so the lines start when it comes). Bubbles stay plain DOM over the canvas (as the Voice's replies, P8.9) rather than CSS2DRenderer quads: same look, one fewer renderer.
- **Journal**: diaries open from J (the selected goblin), the book button in the goblin inspector, or a hut's shelf. The dead keep their diaries (the left page shows a skull). Template text from the Mock carries a small ✎ so the player can tell it from a model's.
- **Pause toast**: at most one every 5 real minutes, so a proxy that is not running does not fill the screen; every failed request still gets the Mock's text.
## P9.5 Books, eulogies, chronicle, village name
- **Books are sim, their words are not**: a book (writer, tick, what it holds, shelf) is sim state, saved and hashed, and reading it really teaches; its title and page are LLM annotations keyed by the book's id, so LLM mode on or off changes nothing in the sim.
- **What goes in a book**: up to three of the writer's teachable recipes and facts that no book holds yet, the ones the fewest goblins know first; the basics everyone grows up with are never written down. One writer at a time, six days' rest between a goblin's books, six books a shelf.
- **Ink**: a page of fiber and ash for ink, taken when the book is finished (from the writer's pack first, then spare stock in the store), so an interrupted writer wastes nothing.
- **The shelf is an idea**: `library_shelf` (4 wood, woodcraft) is known by nobody at the start. Its idea comes like other discoveries at half the usual chance, three times that once something has been lost, to a goblin who knows three things worth passing on in a village of six or more. The village plans one when it has six goblins, every grown one under a finished roof, 4 days of food in store and no shelf with room (without the roof rule, one seed built its shelf on the first day of autumn and had only 70 % of its grown-ups housed at the end of it).
- **Reading waits for food**: reading is a pastime of the curious, so it waits for 4 days of food in store (8 in autumn). Without that, one balance seed read through its autumn, laid in about a fifth less dried and mashed apple, and had 20 starving goblin-days and 2 starvation deaths the next winter (1 goblin-day with no books at all).
- **Recovered knowledge**: `recipe_learned` carries `recovered` when nobody alive knew it, and the feed makes that a critical note ("read back into the village"). A shelf that burns or falls takes its books (`books_lost` with what they held); what was read survives in the readers.
- **Who speaks the eulogy**: the eldest goblin old enough (elder, then adult, then youngling; then age) within 12 tiles of the grave, else the eldest anywhere; when near, its first sentence is spoken over the grave. Its deeds come from the event log (the last 8 days, lines the dead was in, told from the speaker's side, not its death or burial), its family from the living kin's bonds turned to the dead's side ("Mib (child)"). The sim's `grave.eulogy` stays null: the words live in the journals, and the grave inspector shows them.
- **The chronicle**: written on the first day of each season for the season before, by the eldest goblin. Population at its start comes from the last stats sample before it (else end − hatched + died); hatchings, deaths, buildings and discoveries are counted from the event log; the six best village lines are told by the chronicler; lines from diaries are taken only from diaries a real model wrote (the Mock's would only echo the events). The log is not saved, so the first chronicle after loading a save can be thin.
- **The village names itself once**, when its third hut (or stone hut) is finished, in LLM mode only. The founding name stays in the sim (clan name, replays) and the chosen one is what the top bar, village panel, glance and saves show. An answer must be one to three words of letters, at most 24 characters and not a goblin's name; otherwise the Mock's name (parts in either language: "Mossbottom", "Krötenmulde") is used.
- **Book contents in words**: a recipe as "Club: wood and stone" (from its inputs), a fact as "that redcaps are poison" / "dass Rotkappen giftig sind", so a model and the Mock can both build sentences on them.
## P9.6 Language
- **One language setting**: the game's own language (Settings → Language) drives the prompts; there is no separate language for the voices. Instructions stay in English and end "Write in English/German"; every fact in a prompt is already worded in the game's language. What was written keeps the language it was written in when the player switches (journals are not translated), and a request already queued keeps its language.
- **The Mock in both languages**: every product has English and German templates. A test builds each product's prompt in both languages and checks that no English slips into the German text and no German into the English (a bark may be too short to tell). The Voices tab's Test button sends the shared system prompt, so a real model answers in the game's language too.
## P9.7 Divine Voice
- **Words only in LLM mode**: the text box shows when LLM mode is on (the Mock counts: offline, the keyword parser understands the words and template lines answer); with the kill switch off the god speaks in signs only, as the doc's "Divine Signs (always available)".
- **The Mock interprets as the keyword parser does**: the Mock's answer to an interpreter request is the parser's JSON, so a refused, failed or late answer and plain offline play take the same path. The log says which: "read by the language model" only when a real model's answer passed the validator.
- **`nearRef`**: the doc's schema has one `targetRef` per intent, which cannot say "a hut near the pond"; an optional `nearRef` (a place from the refs) is added for build intents, and the sim marks the blueprint out at the best site within the usual radius of that spot (else where it would anyway). The spot travels in the command, so replays need no place names.
- **Dropping what the world cannot take**: each intent is checked with the sim's own validator before the command is pushed; a target it does not know is dropped (the intent kept), an intent that needs a spot it does not have is dropped. Cost and tier problems are left to the command, which the sim refuses with the usual feed note.
- **"Come here" without a place** means where the god's hand points (or the middle of the view).
- **The words stay outside the sim**: the command carries only a key (`textRef`) to the words in `world.llm.speechText` (the last 200 kept), and `god_spoke` passes the key and whether it was a question to the responder.
- **Replies**: the speakers are chosen as for signs (the shaman first for many, up to three); each request carries what it understood, whether it will obey or defy (the sim decided already), its feelings for the god, what it is doing, its last six exchanges, and for a question up to five memories (newest first, with the place's name or a direction from the village, and how long ago). A reply may name the goblin itself and the god; any other goblin's name sends it to the Mock. The god is "the Big Sky" in replies until it has a name.
- **The god's name**: the sim's shaman still names the god deterministically (`@family.temper`); in LLM mode a model is then asked for the village's own name from that name, what the god does most and its temper, checked like the village's name (up to four words, 32 characters). The Mock keeps the shaman's name, so it stays worded in whatever language is shown.


## P9.8 The god in the stories
- **Who tells of the god**: a diary or book names the god only for a believer (faith ≥ the believer line), or, in a diary, for a goblin that spoke with the god that day; the god's name, the goblin's faith in words ("devout, loves the god"; devout from 75 faith, love or fear from 60) and up to two of the day's exchanges go into the prompt. Prayers, answered prayers, the god's words and divine acts were already lines of the day (P9.2); this adds who the god is to the writer. Without the god the prompts are as before, word for word.
- **The chronicle** gets the season's divine numbers (acts, prayers and the answered ones, how often the god spoke) and up to two of the god's own words (from `speechText`), and only when the god did something that season.
- **The god's name in the prompts** is the language model's name if there is one, else the shaman's name in the game's language, else "the Big Sky".
- **German god names** start with an article ("der Gütige Geber"), so the Mock puts the god only where the name stands as the subject, and capitalizes it at a sentence's start.
- **"A Book About You"**: earned when a stored book (title or page) names the god or says "god"/"Gott", from a language model or the Mock (the Mock thanks the god in a believer's book, so the deed can be earned offline in LLM mode). Divine deeds live in the browser profile (`nibblets.ui.deeds`, with when and how it was earned), never in saves; it toasts once. The other deeds and the Codex page are P11.1's.

## P10.1 Rival clans, abstractly
- **`s.rivals` beside `s.clans`**: `s.clans` stays the village's own clans (Bring Forth's stock, lineage stats and the family tree read it); the rival clans beyond the map live in `s.rivals`. When a goblin of a rival clan joins the village (P10.4), its clan joins `s.clans` too.
- **Their own random streams**: the clans are made from a fork of the seed, and each keeps its own stream's state, so they never shift the world's draws (the village's balance is untouched; only entity ids after the founders move up by two or three, which made two timing-fragile tests robust: a beast's closest pass instead of its last position, a sure gut rot).
- **The model**, every 600 ticks: food in goblin-days, gathered by season (spring 1.05, summer 1.25, autumn 1.4, winter 0.55 a goblin a day, ×1.15 for farmers) times the season's luck (0.75–1.2, drawn per clan each season) and `land / pop` once the clan outgrows the land it holds (12–24); births while the store holds 5 days (not in winter), deaths from age and, with an empty store, hunger; never fewer than 5 goblins (a clan does not die out) nor more than 40. Over three years on eight seeds clans hover between 5 and 27, a few go hungry for 2–23 days, and the fierce raid others 1–10 times.
- **Aggression** rests at 0.25 (0.55 for a warlike clan), climbs 0.03 an update while the store holds under 2 days, and calms 0.01 an update. A clan of 0.6 or more that is hungry may raid another rival (8 % an update): it takes 30 % of their store, and each side may lose a goblin.
- **Relation** to the village (−100..100) rests at +15 for traders and −50 per aggression over 0.25, starts within ±30 of that, and drifts 0.4 % of the way back each update (half-life about a month), so what the village does later (P10.2–P10.4 through `shiftRelation`) lasts a while. Words: hostile ≤ −40, wary < −10, neutral < 30, friendly from 30; allies from 70 (only once met), until it falls under 50.
- **First contact** comes after 2–8 days, each next clan 4–12 days later (a `clan_contact` event and a feed note; P10.2 sends the scout in then). Clans not met are only hinted at in the Village panel.
- **What the village knows** of a clan it has met: its colors (skin hues of its gene pool), numbers, stock, the side it lives on, how it fares, what it is known for and its latest news, as if from the scouts' and traders' talk.
- **Save v21**: an old save gets its clans from its seed when loaded, first contact counted from then.

## P10.2 Scouts, traders and trade
- **Visitors are not goblins**: `s.visitors` holds the strangers on the map (a name, a genome bred from their clan's pool, a position and a path, an errand). They are not in `s.goblins`, so needs, mood, the AI, lineage and every count of the village leave them out; they walk their own simple errand (in from the edge toward their clan, stand and stroll 5–9 tiles from the village center, home the same way). The renderer builds them from their genome as grown goblins of that clan's colors, a trader with a basket, a scout with a spear; they can be clicked (their own inspector) but not lifted (yet).
- **Scouts** come once, at first contact, and watch the village for 3–5 hours. **Traders** come every 5–9 days (traders' clans 0.6× as long) while the relation is at least 0, arriving on the 10:00 clan update and leaving at 18:00 (not while a goblin is still on its way to them, for at most an hour more).
- **Offers**: three a visit. A recipe the village does not know (35 %), a trinket (40 %, crafty clans +20 %; rare 1 in 4), the rest stacks of flint, clay, fiber, rope, stone or dried apples. Each asks for something the village has in store, at the clan's greed (1–1.3 times the value). A recipe is priced like an uncommon trinket.
- **Who trades**: a grown goblin that is not hungry, thirsty or tired, when an open offer is worth it by its own reckoning (a thing more when the village has under 3, less over 20; a hoarder values trinkets more; its generosity lets up to half the greed slide). It pays only from what the village can spare: food beyond 6 days, anything else beyond 8 kept. It fetches the price from the store, walks to the trader, takes the goods (a recipe it learns on the spot) and puts things in the store. A deal gives the clan +4 (+2 more when the trader goes home having traded), and food paid fills the clan's store.
- **Goblin with goblin**: a hungry goblin without food gives its best trinket to a goblin it gets on with for as much food as that one reckons the trinket worth; a hoarder gives food for another's trinket. Each trades at most once a day (a small mood lift, +4 opinion both ways).
- **Greetings**: sociable goblins (0.35+) walk over and say hello to a visitor; the first three warm the clan +2 each, so a big welcome is worth +6.
- **Lines**: a trade, a swap and a stranger's coming are diary lines (the clan by its name, goods with their count) and feed notes; a recipe learned in trade is told by the trade line alone.
- **Winter food stays home**: from autumn on the village keeps back all the food it needs to see the winter through (plus 6 days) before it pays a trader in food; a 64-day run on the seed that starved had paid no food at all, so its trouble was elsewhere, but a village should not trade its winter away.

## P10.3 Raids, gifts and defectors
- **Raiders are enemies of a new kind** (`raider`, `enemies/raider.ts`), so alarms, the night watch, `Attack`/`DefendVillage`, fleeing, fences and the danger map all treat them as they treat Gnashers. They carry their clan's id and a genome bred from its pool, and are drawn as grown goblins of that clan's colors with a club and war paint (`render/raiderView.ts`).
- **When**: on a clan's 18:00 update, if the village has met it, it hates the village (−40 or worse), is hungry (under 2 days of food) and fierce (0.5+), it is past day 6, its last raid was 5 or more days ago, and none of its raiders are still about. A quarter of the clan comes, 3 to 6, in at the edge toward their land. The raid itself costs −3 relation.
- **What they do**: make for the store (then for loose food), take 4 food each and walk home, where it fills their clan's store. They are goblins, not beasts: fire does not scare them, they leave stragglers alone, but whoever strikes them gets their club (a swing every 1.4 s, 9 damage, hitting about 60 % less the defender's skill). They run when below 40 % hp or when three armed (or torch-bearing) goblins are round them, and go home after 6 hours whatever happens. One that falls drops its club (raids can arm a village), and its clan is one fewer and −5 more hostile.
- **The chief's gifts**: the chief (only) takes 8 apples' worth of food the village can spare (dried apples first) to the edge of the land toward the coldest clan it has met that is not an ally and is under +30, at most every 4 days per clan. The clan's store fills and it warms by half the food's value (×1.5 if it is hungry), 3 to 12. Scored higher toward a hostile clan and after a raid. Gifts are the way out of a raid spiral: food is what a raiding clan wants.
- **Defectors**: when an exile's time is up it may go over to the clan that likes the village best instead of coming home: 15 % + 35 % × wanderlust + 25 % if it thinks ill of its chief + 20 % if it is unhappy. It walks off the map toward that clan as an exile still; if it cannot get there within a day it gives up and comes home. Gone, it takes what it carries and the trinkets on it; its hut is free, its young are adopted, what only it knew is lost; friends and kin are sad for two days (−8). The clan is one more and its genes join the pool; a clan on good terms warms +4, a hostile one −4 (it hears the village's weaknesses). The lineage book remembers when and where it went (the family tree marks it ↗).

## P10.4 Alliances, marriage migration, the inbreeding penalty
- **Suitors**: on an allied clan's 10:00 update, while the village has an unmated grown goblin (not on its way out) and is under the soft cap, a 30 % chance that it sends one of its goblins to marry in, at most every 4 days per clan. It is a visitor (`kind: 'suitor'`, no pack) that walks in to the village and becomes a goblin of the village there (`alliance.ts` `joinVillage`): its own genome and name, 22–34 days old, of its own clan (which joins `s.clans`, so the lineage panel counts its young), in the lineage book. It brings up to two recipes nobody in the village knows (taken back off the lost list), and comes with a match in mind: the unmated goblin who is most restless, looks most unlike it (skin hue) and is sociable; the two start at +45 opinion and 40 familiarity for each other, so courting does the rest. The clan warms +3.
- **Marrying out**: on the same update each unmated grown goblin with wanderlust 0.65+ (not the chief, not an exile, not held) has a 2 % chance a day to go and marry into the allied clan. It leaves as a defector does (`Defect` then `departGoblin`, `why: 'married'`): genes into the clan's pool, the clan one more and +3, kin and friends sad for two days. A feed note says so; it is not a loss of face as defecting is.
- **Allies' help**: when a Bramblebeast is within 22 tiles of the village, each allied clan's update has a 50 % chance (while none of its warriors are about) to send 2–3 warriors (`kind: 'warrior'`, a spear). They walk to the beast at 1.3× pace and strike it for 12 every 2 s while within 1.8; when it falls (no killer credited to a goblin), flees or a day has passed they walk home. They do not fight Gnashers or raiders: the beast is the danger a small village cannot face alone.
- **Inbreeding**: heterozygosity (the share of loci whose two alleles differ, `genetics/genome.ts`) is the measure. A pair whose mean heterozygosity is under 0.5 lays less: the day's egg chance × (0.5 + 0.5 · h / 0.5), down to half for a fully homozygous pair. Founders are about 0.70–0.77; five generations of sibling pairs get to about 0.3; children of a goblin who married in are back around 0.75. So an isolated village slowly lays less, and the cure is the allies' suitors.
- **Relations drift half as fast** (0.002 of the way home per update instead of 0.004): with the old drift a trading clan levelled off near +60 and seldom crossed 70, so alliances (and with them migration) were rare. The clans still cool toward their nature, only over seasons instead of weeks.
- **A goblin's clan of birth** shows in its inspector ("Born to the Ash clan"); hatchlings take the clan of the parent who laid the egg, as with newcomers' bands (P5).
- **Clan gene pools hold 12 and never self**: a clan's birth now takes two different genomes of its pool (it could pick the same one twice) and the pool keeps 12 instead of 8. With 8 and selfing a pool lost about a tenth of its heterozygosity in 96 days, so its suitors came in more inbred than the village they were meant to refresh; now the pools hold steady at about 0.64–0.71.
- **Diversity is measured three ways** in the balance runner: the living's mean heterozygosity (what the inbreeding penalty reads), Nei's gene diversity of the village (1 − Σp² over binned alleles, `geneDiversity`), and the distinct alleles per locus in a sample of 8 goblins (rarefied, so villages of different sizes compare). The default village is of mixed stock, so at the start it is as diverse as it gets; what isolation costs within 96 days is lost alleles (drift in a village of 10–20), and what migrants bring is alleles of another stock and very heterozygous young.
- **A newcomer is betrothed** (`Relation.match`, both ways): it is matched with an unmated goblin no earlier newcomer is promised to (a 96-day run had four newcomers all promised to one goblin who never wed), suitors come only while someone unpromised is free, it takes a bed in its match's hut when there is one, a betrothed pair may court without a free bed (they find a hut for two after, and a homeless betrothed one looks for room for two), and the promise ends once either weds.
- **The chief seals an alliance, and gives goods when food is short**: a clan from 45 up (not yet allied) is sent a gift too, every few days at most. In five 96-day runs the chief never sent a single gift, because the village never had six days of food to spare; now, with no food to spare, it takes goods worth as much (pelts, rope, flint, clay, fiber, stone, wood or bone, half of what the store holds beyond what it keeps). Relations had hung at 66–69 for a whole run in one seed, and in another a clan sat below 0 (so no traders came) until day 48 and allied on day 89.
- **A newcomer is not matched with one about to grow old** (12 days or more of adulthood left): one newcomer wed a goblin who became an elder within days.
- **A crowded village builds farther out**: when no hut site is left within 14 tiles of the center, the search widens to 20, then 25. Two newcomer couples went a month without a roof because no site was found.
